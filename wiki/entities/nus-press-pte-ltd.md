# NUS Press Pte Ltd

## Overview
NUS Press Pte Ltd is an academic publisher based in Singapore, affiliated with the National University of Singapore. It publishes scholarly works, including books and journals, with a strong focus on Southeast Asia, Asian studies, and various humanities and social science disciplines.

## Role in Context
*   **Publisher of *Southeast of Now***: NUS Press is the publisher of the journal *Southeast of Now: Directions in Contemporary and Modern Art in Asia*, which published [[clare-veal]]'s review of [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History*.
