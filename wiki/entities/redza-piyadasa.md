# Redza Piyadasa

## Overview
Redza Piyadasa (1939–2008) was a prominent Malaysian artist and art critic, recognized for his significant contributions to modern and contemporary art in Southeast Asia. He is mentioned in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History* as a key figure whose earlier work provided "precedents of the Southeast Asian contemporary."

## Key Event Mentioned
*   **1974 Exhibition *Towards a Mystical Reality***: Co-organized with [[sulaiman-esa]], this exhibition is cited as an important development in the earlier period of contemporary art in the region. It represented an artistic tendency towards a "questioning of 'internationalism'" and a turn to social and political context, moving away from hegemonic Western abstraction and formalism.
