# Getty Foundation

## Overview
The Getty Foundation is a philanthropic organization that supports individuals and institutions committed to advancing the understanding and preservation of the visual arts. It provides grants for a wide range of projects, including art historical research, conservation, and professional development.

## Role in Context
*   **Funding Research**: The Getty Foundation funded the [[power-institute]]'s "Ambitious Alignments: New Histories of Southeast Asian Art" research programme, in which [[clare-veal]] participated. This demonstrates the Foundation's support for international scholarship and new approaches to art history, particularly in non-Western contexts.
