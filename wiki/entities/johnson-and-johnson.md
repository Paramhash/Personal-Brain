# Johnson & Johnson ($JNJ$)

Johnson & Johnson is a mega-cap conglomerate with a significant presence in healthcare, including a growing MedTech division that competes in [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|surgical robotics]].

## Rivalry with Medtronic
Through its MedTech division, J&J is developing the *Ottava* robotic platform, positioning itself as a major heavyweight vying for institutional hospital budgets alongside [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]] and [[../entities/intuitive-surgical.md|Intuitive Surgical]]. J&J also holds strong orthopedic robotic anchors via *DePuy Synthes*.

## Business Profile
J&J’s consolidated [[../concepts/operating-margin.md|operating margin]] sits near 25% to 27%. Similar to Medtronic, J&J leverages its massive, diversified portfolio to bundle products. This strategy allows it to absorb lower initial margins on robotic hardware placements, as it captures high-margin recurring revenue on instruments and consumables later.

## Key Segments
*   MedTech (including [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|surgical robotics]] and orthopedics)
*   Pharmaceuticals
*   Consumer Health
