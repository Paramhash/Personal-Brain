# Johann Yamin

[[Johann Yamin]] is a researcher and writer focusing on digital cultures within Singapore and Southeast Asia. His work emphasizes the materiality of communication infrastructures and their connections to colonial histories.

He is co-author of the article "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), which uses [[Media Archaeology]] to explore early [[Internet Art]] in Singapore, including works by [[Lin Hsin Hsin]] and [[tsunamii.net]].

Yamin was a 2020 Rapid Response for a Better Digital Future Fellow at Eyebeam, co-organizing the online project *Pulau Something*. He also served as a Curatorial & Research Resident at the Singapore Art Museum in 2021 and received a Rhizome Microgrant in 2023 to write about Flash-based artworks. He is pursuing an MA in Media Studies at The New School, New York.
