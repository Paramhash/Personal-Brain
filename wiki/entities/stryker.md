# Stryker (SYK)

Stryker is a global medical technology company known for its diverse range of products, including those in orthopedics, medical and surgical, and neurotechnology. In the context of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Stryker is benefiting from an [[../concepts/expanded-patient-funnels-medical-devices.md|expanded patient funnels]] strategy.

GLP-1s are acting as a "pre-habilitation" tool for orthopedic patients. Individuals previously deemed too high-risk for elective joint replacement due to high BMI thresholds are now using GLP-1s to reduce their weight and qualify for surgery. This effectively unlocks a previously unreachable surgical backlog, expanding the market for orthopedic devices. This adaptation is a key part of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
