# University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)

The **University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)** is a public research university in Los Angeles, California. It is a globally recognized institution known for its extensive research activities across various disciplines.

## Research Contributions:

*   **AI in Finance:** UCLA researchers, including [[../entities/yijia-xiao.md|Yijia Xiao]], [[../entities/edward-sun.md|Edward Sun]], [[../entities/di-luo.md|Di Luo]], and [[../entities/wei-wang.md|Wei Wang]], have contributed to cutting-edge research in [[../concepts/llms-in-financial-trading.md|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] for financial applications, such as the [[../entities/tradingagents-framework.md|TradingAgents]] framework.

UCLA faculty and students often collaborate with other academic institutions and research organizations on interdisciplinary projects.
