# Terry Smith

## Overview
Terry Smith is a renowned art historian and theorist whose work on the "contemporary" is referenced in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History*. His theorizations provide a framework for understanding the complexities and characteristics of contemporary art in a global context.

## Key Ideas and Contributions
*   **Theorization of the Contemporary**: Smith's work helps define the nature of contemporary art, particularly its resistance to singular frameworks and its engagement with global dynamics.
*   **Similarity and Difference**: Antoinette draws from Smith's theorization to reject the idea that similarity and difference are mutually exclusive in contemporary art. This informs her methodology, which aims to "think the difference and connection of contemporary Southeast Asian art at once."
