# Margaret Tan

[[Margaret Tan]] is a scholar and artist involved in [[New Media Art]] practices in Singapore. Her work and scholarship often critically examine the intersection of technology, society, and art.

Tan is cited in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) for her concept of "mutated capitalism." She situates Singapore's imagining of technology as an enabler of "limitless possibilities" as an instance of this, where market operations serve as a means of social control, assisted by digital technologies and telecommunications.

She was one of the artists who produced new artworks for the *Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology* exhibition in 2001, curated by [[Gunalan Nadarajan]]. She was also one of the first artists-in-residence at the USP Cyberarts Studio, supporting the production of artworks for the [[Cyberarts]] exhibition.
