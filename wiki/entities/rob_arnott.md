# Rob Arnott

Rob Arnott is a prominent figure in quantitative finance and asset management, widely recognized for his work on fundamental indexing and smart beta strategies. He is also a key contributor to research on robust [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] methodologies.

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/campbell_r_harvey.md|Campbell R. Harvey]] and [[../entities/harry_markowitz.md|Harry Markowitz]], "[[../sources/arnott_harvey_markowitz_2019_backtesting_protocol.md|A backtesting protocol in the era of machine learning]]" (2019). This foundational paper outlines strict protocols to prevent [[../concepts/selection_bias_in_quantitative_models.md|selection bias]], [[../concepts/overfitting_in_quantitative_models.md|overfitting]], and [[../concepts/data_leakage_in_backtesting.md|data-mining]] in quantitative models.
