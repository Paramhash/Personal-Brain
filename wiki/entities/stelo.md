# Stelo

Stelo is a new sensor developed by [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] aimed at the "preventative metabolic health" space. It represents an expansion of [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)]] technology beyond traditional diabetes management.

Stelo is part of [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]]'s strategy to create a lifestyle-tracking subscription ecosystem that complements pharmacological weight management, particularly in the era of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies. This initiative aligns with the broader trend of [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] and the development of [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystems]] within the [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
