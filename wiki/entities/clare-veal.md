# Clare Veal

## Overview
Clare Veal is an academic and art historian specializing in Southeast Asian photography, art, and visual culture, with a particular focus on Thailand. She is a lecturer in the MA Asian Art Histories programme at [[lasalle-college-of-the-arts]] in Singapore.

## Education and Research
*   **PhD**: Received her PhD in 2016 from the Department of Art History and Film Studies at the [[university-of-sydney]]. Her thesis was titled "Thainess Framed: Photography and Thai Identity, 1946–2010".
*   **Research Focus**: Her research primarily explores photography, art, and visual culture in Southeast Asia, particularly Thailand.
*   **Research Programs**: Participated in the [[power-institute]]'s "Ambitious Alignments: New Histories of Southeast Asian Art" research programme, funded by the [[getty-foundation]].

## Publications and Editorial Work
*   **Book Reviews**: Author of the review of Michelle Antoinette's *Reworlding Art History: Contemporary Southeast Asian Art after 1990* in *Southeast of Now: Directions in Contemporary and Modern Art in Asia*.
*   **Journal Contributions**: Has contributed papers to the [[journal-of-aesthetics-and-culture]] and [[trans-asia-photography-review]].
*   **Editorial Role**: Served as the subeditor for "Asian Art" for the [[routledge-encyclopedia-of-modernism]] (2016).
