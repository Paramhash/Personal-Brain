# FreeStyle Libre

The FreeStyle Libre is a continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) system developed by [[../entities/abbott-laboratories.md|Abbott Laboratories]].

## Market Impact
The *FreeStyle Libre* has gained significant market share in diabetes care, putting heavy competitive pressure on [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]]'s own diabetes unit. It represents a key product in Abbott's diversified portfolio.
