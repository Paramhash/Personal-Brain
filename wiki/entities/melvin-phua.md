# Melvin Phua

[[Melvin Phua]] is a scientist and a member of the Singaporean art collective [[tsunamii.net]]. His involvement brings a scientific perspective to the collective's [[Internet Art]] and [[New Media Art]] projects.

As part of [[tsunamii.net]], Phua, alongside artists [[Charles Lim Yi Yong]] and [[Woon Tien Wei]], contributed to works like [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]], a web-based installation exhibited at [[Documenta11]] in 2002. This project involved using technology like GPS to track the artists' physical journey, highlighting the material infrastructure of the internet.

His role in [[tsunamii.net]] is mentioned in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) as part of the collective's interdisciplinary approach to critiquing digital technologies.
