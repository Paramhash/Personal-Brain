# Eli Lilly and Company (LLY)

Eli Lilly and Company is a major American pharmaceutical company. In recent years, it has become a dominant force in the healthcare sector, largely due to its success in the [[../concepts/GLP-1_Receptor_Agonists.md|GLP-1 receptor agonist]] market.

## Key GLP-1 Products

*   **Mounjaro:** Originally approved for type 2 diabetes, also used off-label for weight loss.
*   **Zepbound:** Approved specifically for chronic weight management.

## Impact on XLV

The astronomical success of Mounjaro and Zepbound has propelled Eli Lilly to become the undisputed heavyweight champion within the [[../entities/XLV.md|Health Care Select Sector SPDR Fund (XLV)]]. It currently commands over 16% of the entire fund, significantly influencing XLV's overall valuation and performance. This concentration makes XLV's short-to-medium-term alpha heavily tethered to Eli Lilly's pipeline, manufacturing capabilities, and regulatory outcomes.

## Valuation Concerns

Eli Lilly's blistering rally, driven by GLP-1 success, has pushed its trailing Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio well past historic pharmaceutical norms. This premium contributes to [[../entities/XLV.md|XLV]]'s overall valuation distortion, making the ETF more sensitive to growth-stock volatility.
