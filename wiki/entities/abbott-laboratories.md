# Abbott Laboratories ($ABT$)

Abbott Laboratories is a diversified healthcare company and a close direct competitor to [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]].

## Rivalry with Medtronic
Abbott competes with Medtronic across various segments, including structural heart valves and diabetes care. Its *[[../entities/freestyle-libre.md|FreeStyle Libre]]* product places significant pressure on Medtronic's diabetes unit.

## Business Profile
Abbott operates with a highly stable [[../concepts/operating-margin.md|operating margin]] of ~22% to 24%. Its structural diversification across nutrition, diagnostics, and pharmaceuticals insulates its margins from single-sector shocks, although its pure medical device segments often exhibit higher standalone profitability.

## Key Segments
*   [[../concepts/cardiovascular-care.md|Cardiovascular Care]]
*   Diabetes Care (e.g., [[../entities/freestyle-libre.md|FreeStyle Libre]])
*   Nutrition
*   Diagnostics
*   Pharmaceuticals
