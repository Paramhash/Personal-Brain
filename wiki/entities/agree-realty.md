# Agree Realty ($ADC)

[[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]] is a [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owner]] and a [[../concepts/high-fidelity-credit-citadel.md|High-Fidelity Credit Citadel]] within the [[../concepts/bulletproof-land-bank.md|Bulletproof Land Bank]] investment thesis.

## Key Characteristics:
*   **Portfolio Quality:** Operates one of the highest-credit-quality portfolios in the net-lease space.
*   **Tenant Base:** Approximately 68% of annualized base rent is generated by [[../concepts/investment-grade-tenants.md|investment-grade tenants]] such as [[../entities/walmart.md|Walmart]], [[../entities/tractor-supply.md|Tractor Supply]], and [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]].
*   **Balance Sheet:** Maintains a pristine balance sheet, enabling opportunistic acquisition of cash-flowing assets even in tight capital environments, widening [[../concepts/cost-of-capital-spreads.md|investment spreads]].
*   **Occupancy Profile:** Ultra-stable, with an expected ~99.7% occupancy by mid-2026.
*   **Dividend Yield:** Expected ~4.25% by mid-2026.
*   **Current Mispricing:** Locked in a flat range due to macro institutional outflows from the Real Estate sector, mispricing its growth engine.
*   **Value Catalysts:** Structural rotation into defensive income assets and steady mid-single-digit [[../concepts/funds-from-operations.md|funds from operations]] (FFO) growth.
