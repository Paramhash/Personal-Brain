# Journal of Risk / Journal of Risk Model Validation

"Journal of Risk" and "Journal of Risk Model Validation" are two distinct but complementary academic journals that provide excellent resources for understanding the structural traps of [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]], [[../concepts/tail_risk_modeling.md|tail-risk modeling]], and stress-testing derivatives strategies. They focus on the practical challenges and methodologies for robust risk management and model assessment.

**Core Focus Areas:**
*   Risk measurement and management techniques (e.g., [[../concepts/conditional_value_at_risk.md|CVaR]], GEX)
*   Model validation methodologies and best practices
*   Stress testing and scenario analysis for financial portfolios
*   Regulatory aspects of risk management
*   Empirical studies on financial risk, especially for derivatives
*   Operational risk and systemic risk

These journals are particularly valuable for researchers and practitioners concerned with the robustness and reliability of quantitative models, especially in the context of complex instruments like options, where [[../concepts/transaction_costs_in_options.md|transaction costs]] and [[../concepts/liquidity_modeling_in_options.md|liquidity]] can significantly impact real-world performance.
