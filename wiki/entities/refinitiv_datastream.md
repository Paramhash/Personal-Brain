# Refinitiv Datastream

## Definition
[[../entities/refinitiv_datastream.md|Refinitiv Datastream]] (formerly Thomson Reuters Datastream) is a global financial database that provides historical financial market data, economic data, and news. It offers extensive coverage across various asset classes, including equities, fixed income, commodities, and foreign exchange, as well as macroeconomic indicators.

## Products and Services
*   **Comprehensive Data:** Access to a vast array of historical and real-time financial data series.
*   **Analytical Tools:** Integrated tools for data analysis, charting, and reporting.
*   **Economic Indicators:** Provides macroeconomic data essential for economic and financial modeling.

## Role in Research
[[../entities/refinitiv_datastream.md|Refinitiv Datastream]] is a common source for researchers needing reliable historical financial and economic data. In [[../sources/detecting_stock_market_regimes_lai_2022.md|Lai (2022)]], it was used to collect the one-month US TBill rate (as a proxy for the [[../concepts/risk_free_rate.md|risk-free rate]]) and index dividend yields, which are crucial inputs for calculating [[../concepts/equity_risk_premium.md|equity risk premium]] and for the forecasting application.
