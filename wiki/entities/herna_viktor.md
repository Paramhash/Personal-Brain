# Herna Viktor

Herna Viktor is a researcher specializing in machine learning and its applications in financial markets, particularly in the context of [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] and systematic surveys.

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/kenniy_olorunnimbe.md|Kenniy Olorunnimbe]], "[[../sources/olorunnimbe_viktor_2022_deep_learning_in_stock_market.md|Deep learning in the stock market—a systematic survey of practice, backtesting, and applications]]" (2022). This paper provides a structured taxonomy for evaluating complex quantitative models using historical market data.
