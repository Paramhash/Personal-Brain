# Michelle Antoinette

## Overview
Michelle Antoinette is an art historian and author, best known for her work on contemporary Southeast Asian art. Her seminal book, *Reworlding Art History: Contemporary Southeast Asian Art after 1990* (2014), introduces and develops the concept of "reworlding" as a critical framework for understanding global art histories.

## Key Contributions
*   **Reworlding Art History**: Antoinette posits "reworlding" as a theoretical approach that challenges Euro-American-centric narratives in art history. It involves understanding how art worlds are formulated from diverse, non-Euro-American perspectives, thereby disrupting homogenizing and essentializing narratives.
*   **Focus on Southeast Asian Art**: Her work uses contemporary Southeast Asian art after 1990 as a paradigmatic example to illustrate the processes of "reworlding." She examines how artists in the region negotiate localization and globalization, and how their practices resist reduction to identity politics or culturalist interpretations.
*   **Critique of Art Historical Frameworks**: Antoinette critiques the limitations of traditional art historical tools in addressing the heterogeneity of contemporary art, particularly in non-Western contexts. She advocates for methodologies that can simultaneously account for both the specificities and connections within global art.

## Key Works
*   *Reworlding Art History: Contemporary Southeast Asian Art after 1990* (Brill, 2014)
*   Co-editor of *Contemporary Asian Art and Exhibitions* (ANU Press, 2014)
