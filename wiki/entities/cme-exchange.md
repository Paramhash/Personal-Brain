# CME Exchange

The Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME) is a global derivatives marketplace. It offers a wide range of futures and options products across various asset classes, including interest rates, equities, foreign exchange, and commodities. CME is a major provider of [[../concepts/span-margin.md|SPAN margin data]] for futures and futures options.
