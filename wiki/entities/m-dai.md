# M. Dai

## Role
Co-author of the paper "Trend Following Trading under a Regime Switching Model" (2010).

## Affiliation
At the time of publication, M. Dai was affiliated with:
- Department of Mathematics, National University of Singapore (NUS)
- Risk Management Institute, NUS

## Research Interests (inferred from source)
- Financial mathematics
- Optimal stopping problems
- Regime switching models
- Transaction costs in financial markets
- Stochastic control and optimization

## Key Contributions in Source
- Developed a theoretical framework for optimal [[Trend Following Trading]] using [[Regime Switching Model (Financial)]] and [[Optimal Stopping Time (Finance)]].
- Applied the [[Wonham Filter]] to handle unobservable market regimes.
- Contributed to the mathematical derivation of optimal buy/sell thresholds via [[Double Obstacle Problem (Finance)]].
- Conducted numerical experiments and market tests to validate the proposed strategy.
