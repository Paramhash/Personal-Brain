# Intuitive Surgical ($ISRG$)

Intuitive Surgical is the undisputed market leader in [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|robotic-assisted surgery]], primarily known for its *[[../entities/da-vinci-surgical-system.md|da Vinci]]* ecosystem.

## Market Position
Intuitive Surgical has maintained a dominant position as a pure-play monopoly in the surgical robotics market for over two decades. Its *[[../entities/da-vinci-surgical-system.md|da Vinci]]* system boasts a massive installed base, creating a significant competitive moat.

## Rivalry with Medtronic
Intuitive Surgical is the primary competitor to [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]] in the [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|surgical robotics]] space. Medtronic's [[../entities/hugo-robotic-system.md|Hugo robotic system]] aims to challenge Intuitive's dominance by offering a modular, cart-based alternative at a slightly lower initial price point.

## Margin Profile
As a pure-play monopoly, Intuitive Surgical boasts elite, software-like margins. It maintains a commanding [[../concepts/gross-margin.md|gross margin]] of ~67% to 68% and an [[../concepts/operating-margin.md|operating margin]] of ~30% to 33%. This high profitability is characteristic of a successful [[../concepts/razor-and-blade-business-model.md|razor-and-blade business model]], where recurring revenue from instruments and consumables drives significant profits.

For a broader view of the competitive landscape, refer to [[../research/medtronics-competitive-landscape.md|Medtronic's Competitive Landscape]].
