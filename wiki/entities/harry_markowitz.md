# Harry Markowitz

Harry Markowitz is a Nobel laureate in Economic Sciences, widely regarded as the "father of Modern Portfolio Theory" for his pioneering work on [[../concepts/mean_variance_optimization.md|mean-variance optimization]]. His contributions laid the groundwork for quantitative portfolio management. Beyond his foundational work, he has also contributed to the methodology of robust quantitative research, including [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]].

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/rob_arnott.md|Rob Arnott]] and [[../entities/campbell_r_harvey.md|Campbell R. Harvey]], "[[../sources/arnott_harvey_markowitz_2019_backtesting_protocol.md|A backtesting protocol in the era of machine learning]]" (2019). This paper provides strict protocols to prevent common biases in backtesting quantitative models.
