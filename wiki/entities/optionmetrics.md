# OptionMetrics

## Definition
[[../entities/optionmetrics.md|OptionMetrics]] is a leading provider of historical [[../concepts/options.md|options]] data and analytics for institutional investors and academic researchers. Its IvyDB database is a comprehensive source of end-of-day and intraday options data, including implied volatilities, Greeks, and other derived metrics.

## Products and Services
*   **IvyDB:** A flagship product offering high-quality, clean, and comprehensive historical options data across various exchanges.
*   **Analytics:** Provides tools and analytics for options research, trading, and risk management.

## Role in Research
[[../entities/optionmetrics.md|OptionMetrics]] data is widely used in academic studies that require detailed and reliable options information. For example, [[../sources/detecting_stock_market_regimes_lai_2022.md|Lai (2022)]] utilized daily options data from OptionMetrics (along with [[../entities/cboe_global_markets.md|CBOE Global Markets]]) to extract [[../concepts/option_implied_equity_risk_premium.md|option-implied equity risk premia]] for detecting [[../concepts/stock_market_regimes.md|stock market regimes]]. The quality and breadth of its data are critical for accurately calculating implied variances and other option-derived measures.
