# Wan Ni Lai

## Affiliation
Skema Business School, Université Côte d’Azur, France

## Research Focus
Wan Ni Lai is a researcher primarily focused on financial markets, particularly in the areas of:
*   **Regime Switching Models**: Investigating methods to detect and model shifts in financial market regimes.
*   **Option Pricing**: Utilizing information embedded in option prices for financial analysis.
*   **Equity Risk Premium**: Studying the estimation and behavior of the equity risk premium.
*   **Market Forecasting**: Applying advanced models to improve predictions of market behavior.

## Key Contributions
In the paper "[[detecting-stock-market-regimes-from-option-prices-lai-2022|Detecting Stock Market Regimes from Option Prices]]" (2022), Lai demonstrates that:
*   Forward-looking information from option prices, specifically the [[horizon-spread-option-implied-erp|horizon spread]] of option-implied [[equity-risk-premium|equity risk premia]], significantly enhances the detection of [[stock-market-regimes|stock market regimes]].
*   This approach provides earlier and sharper signals of regime switches compared to traditional methods based on historical returns or conditional volatility.
*   The improved regime detection leads to better forecasting performance for the equity premium across various market conditions and geographies (US and Emerging Markets).
