# Tauric Research

**Tauric Research** is a research organization involved in the development of advanced AI systems, particularly in the domain of [[../concepts/llms-in-financial-trading.md|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] for financial applications.

## Key Contributions:

*   **[[../entities/tradingagents-framework.md|TradingAgents]] Framework:** Tauric Research is a key contributor to the development of the TradingAgents framework, a novel [[../concepts/multi-agent-llm-financial-trading.md|multi-agent LLM financial trading system]]. This framework aims to simulate the collaborative dynamics of real-world trading firms using specialized LLM-powered agents.

## Affiliations:

Researchers from Tauric Research, including [[../entities/yijia-xiao.md|Yijia Xiao]] and [[../entities/edward-sun.md|Edward Sun]], have collaborated with academics from institutions like [[../entities/university-of-california-los-angeles.md|University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)]] and [[../entities/massachusetts-institute-of-technology.md|Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)]] on projects like TradingAgents.
