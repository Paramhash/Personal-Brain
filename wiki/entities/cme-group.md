# CME Group

CME Group is one of the world's leading and most diverse derivatives marketplaces. It offers a wide range of futures and options products across all major asset classes, including interest rates, equity indexes, foreign exchange, energy, agricultural commodities, and metals.

Market sessions for CME Group products are covered by the `CME` [[../concepts/instrument-collections.md|instrument collection]] within the [[../concepts/market-sessions.md|Tastyworks Market Sessions API]].
