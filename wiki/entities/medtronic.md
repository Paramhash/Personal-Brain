# Medtronic (MDT)

Medtronic is one of the world's largest medical technology companies, offering a wide range of products and therapies, including those for cardiovascular health. In the context of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Medtronic is aligning with a [[../concepts/high-acuity-innovation-medical-devices.md|high-acuity innovation]] strategy.

Medtronic recognizes that GLP-1s, while beneficial for systemic cardiovascular risk reduction, do not address structural heart issues or complex calcified arterial blockages. Consequently, the company is focusing its R&D on advanced cardiovascular interventions and structural heart technologies. These devices are designed for patients with complex, advanced disease that necessitates mechanical intervention irrespective of their metabolic state or GLP-1 usage. This strategic pivot is a key component of the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
