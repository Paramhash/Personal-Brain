# Hugo Robotic System

The Hugo robotic system is [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]]'s entry into the [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|robotic-assisted surgery]] market, designed to compete with established platforms like the [[../entities/da-vinci-surgical-system.md|da Vinci Surgical System]].

## Features and Market Position
*   **Design**: Modular and cart-based, offering flexibility in operating room setup.
*   **Price Point**: Positioned at a slightly lower initial price point ($1M–$2M) compared to the *da Vinci* system ($1.5M–$2.5M).
*   **Target Market**: Aims to make inroads in ambulatory centers and international markets where cost and modularity can be significant advantages.

The Hugo system represents Medtronic's strategy to challenge the market dominance of [[../entities/intuitive-surgical.md|Intuitive Surgical]] in the soft-tissue robotics space.
