# Nutrisense

Nutrisense is a company acquired by [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] that specializes in lifestyle-tracking subscription ecosystems, often utilizing [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|CGM]] data to provide personalized metabolic insights.

The acquisition of Nutrisense is a strategic move by [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] to expand its offerings into the "preventative metabolic health" space and create a comprehensive [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystem]]. This integration is designed to complement pharmacological weight management, particularly for users of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies. Nutrisense plays a role in [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]], a key aspect of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
