# Novo Nordisk (NVO)

Novo Nordisk is a global pharmaceutical company headquartered in Denmark, renowned for its leadership in diabetes care and, more recently, chronic weight management through [[../concepts/GLP-1_Receptor_Agonists.md|GLP-1 receptor agonists]].

## Key GLP-1 Products

*   **Ozempic:** Primarily for type 2 diabetes.
*   **Wegovy:** Approved for chronic weight management.

## Exclusion from XLV

Despite being a global titan in the GLP-1 market, Novo Nordisk is entirely excluded from the [[../entities/XLV.md|Health Care Select Sector SPDR Fund (XLV)]]. This is because XLV only tracks S&P 500 constituents, and Novo Nordisk is a Danish company. Consequently, XLV's exposure to the multi-billion-dollar GLP-1 thesis is almost entirely consolidated into [[../entities/Eli_Lilly.md|Eli Lilly]].
