# Abbott (ABT)

Abbott is a diversified global healthcare company with a significant presence in medical devices, including [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs)]]. In the context of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Abbott, like [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]], is actively positioning its CGMs as essential tools for users.

Abbott's strategy aligns with the [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] approach, emphasizing the role of CGMs in monitoring metabolic health and tracking the efficacy of GLP-1 drug regimens. This contributes to the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]], where devices are seen as complementary rather than competitive to new pharmacotherapies.
