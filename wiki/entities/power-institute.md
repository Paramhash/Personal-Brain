# Power Institute

## Overview
The Power Institute is a center for art and visual culture at the University of Sydney, Australia. It supports research, publications, and public programs that explore the history, theory, and criticism of art.

## Role in Context
*   **Research Program**: [[clare-veal]], the author of the review article, was a participant in the Power Institute's "Ambitious Alignments: New Histories of Southeast Asian Art" research programme from 2015–16. This program was funded by the [[getty-foundation]], highlighting the institute's role in fostering international collaborative research on Southeast Asian art history.
