# Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

The **Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)** is a private research university in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It is renowned globally for its leadership in science, engineering, and technology, and its significant contributions to research and innovation.

## Research Contributions:

*   **AI in Finance:** MIT researchers, including [[../entities/di-luo.md|Di Luo]], have been involved in advanced research applying [[../entities/large-language-models-llm.md|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] to complex domains like financial trading, contributing to frameworks such as [[../entities/tradingagents-framework.md|TradingAgents]].

## Affiliations:

MIT fosters a highly collaborative research environment, engaging with other top universities and industry partners on a wide array of scientific and technological advancements.
