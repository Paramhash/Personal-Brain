# Dollar General ($DG)

[[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]] is a [[../concepts/structural-retail-operators.md|structural retail operator]] primarily focused on rural communities. It is characterized as a [[../concepts/deep-value-rural-monopolist.md|Deep Value Rural Monopolist]] within the [[../concepts/squeezed-wallet-flywheel.md|Squeezed-Wallet Flywheel]] investment thesis.

## Key Characteristics:
*   **Real Estate Strategy:** Employs [[../concepts/geographic-insulation.md|geographic insulation]], with over 75% of its ~20,000 locations in rural areas (populations under 20,000).
*   **Market Role:** Serves as the default local infrastructure for consumables in its operating areas.
*   **Customer Base:** Benefits from [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|middle/low-income trade-down traffic]] when households experience cost-of-living distress.
*   **Current Mispricing:** Wall Street has discounted the equity due to [[../concepts/temporary-margin-friction.md|temporary margin friction]] stemming from reduced federal SNAP benefits.
*   **Value Catalysts:** Stabilization of [[../concepts/supply-chain-input-costs.md|supply chain input costs]] and expansion of high-margin [[../concepts/digital-private-label-expansion.md|digital/private-label expansion]].
