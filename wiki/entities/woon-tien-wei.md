# Woon Tien Wei

[[Woon Tien Wei]] is a Singaporean artist and a member of the art collective [[tsunamii.net]]. He is involved in the collective's [[Internet Art]] and [[New Media Art]] projects, which often explore the physical and political dimensions of digital networks.

As part of [[tsunamii.net]], Woon Tien Wei, along with [[Charles Lim Yi Yong]] and [[Melvin Phua]], created [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]], an installation exhibited at [[Documenta11]] in 2002. This work aimed to disrupt the perceived efficiency of the internet by having the artists physically travel to a web server's location. Woon has also written about how events like the dotcom crash influenced the collective's formation, noting that the anticipated hype did not fully materialize in Singapore despite government preparations.

His contributions to [[tsunamii.net]] are discussed in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) as part of a critical examination of early internet art in Singapore.
