# FINRA (Financial Industry Regulatory Authority)

FINRA is a private, self-regulatory organization (SRO) that oversees brokerage firms and their registered brokers in the United States. Authorized by Congress, FINRA's mission is to protect investors by ensuring that the U.S. securities industry operates fairly and honestly.

FINRA develops and enforces rules governing the activities of all registered broker-dealer firms and their millions of registered employees in the U.S. One notable rule set by FINRA is the [[../concepts/pattern-day-trader.md|Pattern Day Trader (PDT)]] rule.
