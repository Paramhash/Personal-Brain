# Gregoire Loeper

Gregoire Loeper is a researcher in quantitative finance, with contributions to the field of systematic options strategies and [[../concepts/option_overlays.md|option overlays]].

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/ivan_guo.md|Ivan Guo]], "[[../sources/guo_loeper_2020_designing_all_weather_overlays.md|Designing All-Weather Overlays — A Study on Option-Based Systematic Strategies]]" (2020). This paper focuses on the empirical design of systematic options selling strategies combined with downside protection.
