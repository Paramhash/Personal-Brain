# NNN Realty ($NNN)

[[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]] is a [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owner]] and a [[../concepts/pure-play-yield-engine.md|Pure-Play Yield Engine]] within the [[../concepts/bulletproof-land-bank.md|Bulletproof Land Bank]] investment thesis.

## Key Characteristics:
*   **Asset Focus:** Concentrates entirely on [[../concepts/single-tenant-retail-properties.md|single-tenant retail properties]] leased to stable regional operators.
*   **Lease Structure:** Utilizes ultra-long-term [[../concepts/triple-net-structures.md|triple-net structures]].
*   **Dividend History:** Boasts a 30-plus year history of consecutive annual [[../concepts/dividend-increases.md|dividend increases]].
*   **Occupancy Profile:** Ultra-stable, with an expected ~99.4% occupancy by mid-2026.
*   **Dividend Yield:** Expected ~5.29% by mid-2026.
*   **Balance Sheet:** Characterized by [[../concepts/balance-sheet-playbook.md|long-term fixed debt with zero near-term maturities]].
*   **Current Mispricing:** The market treats NNN like a flat corporate bond, overlooking the high [[../concepts/replacement-value.md|replacement value]] of its physical real estate and its near-perfect [[../concepts/rent-collection-history.md|rent collection history]].
*   **Value Catalysts:** Short-term macro rate stabilization is expected to remove the "bond proxy" penalty, triggering an upward re-rating to [[../concepts/net-asset-value.md|Net Asset Value]] (NAV).
