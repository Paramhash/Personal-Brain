# Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology (2001)

*Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology* was a significant exhibition held in 2001 as part of the Nokia Singapore Art festival. It was curated by [[Gunalan Nadarajan]] and featured works of [[Internet Art]], game art, and multimedia installations.

The exhibition was described as an explicit attempt to "jumpstart" the systematic development of [[New Media Art]] practice in Singapore by facilitating the local production of exemplary works in the field of [[Cyberarts]]. It coincided with a flurry of activity for new media art in Singapore, including the launch of the National Arts Council's New Media Arts Fund and the establishment of the Cyberarts/Cyberculture Research Initiative (CCRI) at NUS.

Works by artists like [[Margaret Tan]], [[Irina Aristarkhova]], and the internet art collective [[tsunamii.net]] (which included an early iteration of their *alpha* series, *alpha 3.3*) were featured.

"[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) highlights this exhibition as a key moment in the emergence of institutional support for [[New Media Art]] in Singapore, despite the "paucity" of artists working with technology at the time.

## Notes
The *Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology* exhibition was a pivotal event that attempted to formalize and promote new media art in Singapore, laying groundwork for future developments and discussions.
