# Documenta11

[[Documenta11]] was a major international contemporary art exhibition held in Kassel, Germany, in 2002. It is significant for its global scope and its role in shaping discourses around art.

The exhibition featured the web-based installation and performance [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]] by the Singaporean art collective [[tsunamii.net]] ([[Charles Lim Yi Yong]], [[Woon Tien Wei]], [[Melvin Phua]]). This inclusion affirmed the pertinence of [[New Media Art]] practices for Singapore on an international stage.

"[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) discusses [[Documenta11]] as a platform where [[Internet Art]] gained canonical importance, yet also faced mixed reception due to its technological nature. The article highlights how [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]] functioned as an institutional critique of mega-exhibitions like Documenta, particularly regarding the geographical consolidation of intellectual resources and potential "cultural colonisation."

## Notes
[[Documenta11]]'s inclusion of [[tsunamii.net]]'s work underscores the growing, albeit complex, recognition of [[Internet Art]] within mainstream international art institutions, while also providing a context for critical artistic interventions.
