# Ivan Guo

Ivan Guo is a researcher known for his work in quantitative finance, particularly in the area of systematic options strategies and [[../concepts/option_overlays.md|option overlays]].

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/gregoire_loeper.md|Gregoire Loeper]], "[[../sources/guo_loeper_2020_designing_all_weather_overlays.md|Designing All-Weather Overlays — A Study on Option-Based Systematic Strategies]]" (2020). This paper provides an empirical template for structural design, examining systematic options selling strategies paired with downside protection.
