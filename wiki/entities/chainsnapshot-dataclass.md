# ChainSnapshot Dataclass

The `ChainSnapshot` dataclass is a structured data object that encapsulates a snapshot of the entire options chain for a given underlying at a specific point in time. It typically contains comprehensive information for all available options contracts (calls and puts) across various strike prices and expiration dates.

**Typical Contents:**
*   Underlying price
*   Contract details (strike, expiration, type - call/put)
*   Bid/Ask prices
*   Mid-price
*   Volume
*   Open Interest (OI)
*   Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta, Rho)
*   Implied Volatility (IV)

In the [[../concepts/near-expiry-hmm-options-dynamics.md|Near-Expiry HMM for SPX/SPY Options Dynamics]], `ChainSnapshot` objects are the primary input for live inference. They are generated by `data_engine.py` (consuming data from [[../entities/dxfeed.md|dxFeed]]) and are used by `hmm/features.py` to compute the 7 feature vectors required by the [[../concepts/gaussian-hmm.md|GaussianHMM]].
