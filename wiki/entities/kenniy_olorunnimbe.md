# Kenniy Olorunnimbe

Kenniy Olorunnimbe is a researcher in the field of machine learning applications in finance, with a focus on [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] methodologies and systematic reviews.

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/herna_viktor.md|Herna Viktor]], "[[../sources/olorunnimbe_viktor_2022_deep_learning_in_stock_market.md|Deep learning in the stock market—a systematic survey of practice, backtesting, and applications]]" (2022). This comprehensive survey is valuable for its breakdown of professional-grade backtesting methodologies, execution traps, and [[../concepts/data_leakage_in_backtesting.md|data leakage]] issues.
