# Cboe Futures Exchange (CFE)

The Cboe Futures Exchange (CFE) is a futures exchange operated by Cboe Global Markets. It is primarily known for listing futures contracts on the VIX Index (Volatility Index), which is a key measure of market expectations of near-term volatility conveyed by S&P 500 stock index option prices.

Market sessions for CFE products are covered by the `CFE` [[../concepts/instrument-collections.md|instrument collection]] within the [[../concepts/market-sessions.md|Tastyworks Market Sessions API]].
