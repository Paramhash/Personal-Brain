# Ocarina (1994)

[[Ocarina (1994)]] is a three-dimensional digital art object created by Singaporean artist [[Lin Hsin Hsin]]. It was exhibited in the Electronic Gallery of the [[Lin Hsin Hsin Art Museum]], her pioneering virtual museum, with an earliest archival date of 1997.

The artwork is presented as a static .gif image, depicting a discoidal form with a metallic texture, scintillant with simulated light in hues from magenta to cobalt, set against a black background. Its description in the virtual museum includes details like "24 bit RGB true color in 300 dpi," making it comparable to traditional art mediums.

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), [[Ocarina (1994)]] is analyzed as an early instance of [[Internet Art]] in Singapore. It embodies a "modernist" approach, focusing "on" the digital medium itself and celebrating the "magic" of technological apparatuses like the PC mouse, aligning with Singapore's [[Intelligent Island]] rhetoric of the time. The work emerged at the cusp of the internet's integration into everyday life in Singapore.
