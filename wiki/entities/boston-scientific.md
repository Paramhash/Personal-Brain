# Boston Scientific (BSX)

Boston Scientific is a global medical technology leader providing a broad range of high-performance solutions for various medical conditions, including cardiovascular diseases. In response to [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Boston Scientific is focusing on a [[../concepts/high-acuity-innovation-medical-devices.md|high-acuity innovation]] strategy.

While GLP-1s reduce systemic cardiovascular risk, they do not resolve structural heart issues such as severe valve stenosis or complex calcified arterial blockages. Boston Scientific's R&D is therefore shifting towards advanced structural heart technologies, including [[../concepts/intravascular-lithotripsy-ivl.md|Intravascular Lithotripsy (IVL)]]. These devices target patients with complex, advanced disease that requires mechanical intervention regardless of their metabolic state or GLP-1 usage. This strategic focus is a key part of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
