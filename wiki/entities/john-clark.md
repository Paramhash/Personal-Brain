# John Clark

## Overview
John Clark is a prominent scholar and art historian whose writings on "worlding" are referenced in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History*. His work contributes to understanding the global dimensions of art history, particularly in Asian contexts.

## Key Ideas and Contributions
*   **"Worlding"**: Clark's concept of "worlding" is aligned with Antoinette's "reworlding," affirming the 1990s as a period when "interpretative frames to art discourses that are visible in a global perspective across cultural and temporal zones" began to emerge, challenging previous Euro-American domination.
*   **Asian Modern Art**: He has extensively researched and written on modern and contemporary Asian art, contributing to a more inclusive and global understanding of art history.

## Key Works Mentioned
*   "The Worlding of the Asian Modern". In *Contemporary Asian Art and Exhibitions: Connectivities and World-Making*, ed. Michelle Antoinette and Caroline Turner. Canberra: ANU Press, 2014.
