# Zimmer Biomet (ZBH)

Zimmer Biomet is a global medical technology leader focused on musculoskeletal healthcare, primarily in orthopedics. Similar to [[../entities/stryker.md|Stryker]], Zimmer Biomet is experiencing an [[../concepts/expanded-patient-funnels-medical-devices.md|expanded patient funnels]] effect due to the rise of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies.

GLP-1s are serving as a "pre-habilitation" tool, enabling patients with high BMIs to reduce their weight and meet the criteria for elective joint replacement surgeries. This phenomenon is unlocking a previously inaccessible surgical backlog, thereby expanding the market for Zimmer Biomet's orthopedic devices. This strategic benefit is a key aspect of the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
