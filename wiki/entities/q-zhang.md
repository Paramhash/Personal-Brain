# Q. Zhang

## Role
Co-author of the paper "Trend Following Trading under a Regime Switching Model" (2010).

- Department of Mathematics, University of Georgia, Athens, GA

## Research Interests (inferred from source)
- Financial mathematics

- Stochastic control and optimization
- Filtering techniques in finance

- Contributed to the theoretical justification of [[Trend Following Trading]] strategies.
- Played a role in applying [[Regime Switching Model (Financial)]] and [[Optimal Stopping Time (Finance)]] to financial markets.
- Involved in the use of the [[Wonham Filter]] for unobservable market states.
- Participated in the mathematical analysis leading to the [[Double Obstacle Problem (Finance)]] for trading thresholds.
