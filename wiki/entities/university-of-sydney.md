# University of Sydney

## Overview
The University of Sydney is a public research university located in Sydney, Australia. It is one of Australia's oldest and most prestigious universities, offering a wide array of academic programs and research opportunities across various faculties.

## Role in Context

Her thesis was titled "Thainess Framed: Photography and Thai Identity, 1946–2010."
