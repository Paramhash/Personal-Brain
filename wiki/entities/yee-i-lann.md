# Yee I-Lann

## Overview
Yee I-Lann is a contemporary artist from Southeast Asia whose work is cited in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History* as an example of how artists negotiate complex subjectivities within the region and globally. Her practice often involves photography and digital media to explore historical narratives, cultural identity, and geopolitical issues.

## Key Work Mentioned
*   ***Sulu Stories*** (2005): This is a series of photomontages that represent dramatizations of tales about Sulu, a geographical area located between Malaysia and the Philippines.
    *   **Interpretation**: As discussed by [[clare-veal]] in her review of Antoinette's book, *Sulu Stories* acts as an "illustrative metaphor" for the dynamic and complex nature of artistic subjectivities in Southeast Asia and their negotiation with the rest of the world. It highlights the intricate cultural and historical connections within the region.
