# Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis (JFQA)

The Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis (JFQA) is a highly respected academic journal that focuses heavily on quantitative approaches to asset management, empirical finance, and risk frameworks. It publishes rigorous research that contributes to the understanding of financial markets and decision-making.

**Core Focus Areas:**
*   Asset pricing
*   Corporate finance
*   Investments
*   Financial institutions
*   Quantitative methods in finance
*   Empirical studies using financial data

While not exclusively dedicated to derivatives, it frequently features papers that apply quantitative methods to [[../concepts/options_portfolio_optimization.md|options portfolio optimization]], [[../concepts/risk_management.md|risk management]], and other derivatives-related topics.
