# Lani Maestro

## Overview
Lani Maestro is a contemporary artist whose work is highlighted in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History* as an example of artistic subjectivities in Southeast Asia. Her installations often engage with themes of identity, memory, and human experience.

## Key Work Mentioned
*   ***a book thick of ocean*** (1993): This is a participatory installation consisting of a small wooden table upon which a large book is placed. As the audience leafs through its pages, they discover unidentifiable photographs of the ocean.
    *   **Interpretation**: As discussed by [[clare-veal]] in her review of Antoinette's book, this work serves as an "illustrative metaphor" for the dynamic and complex nature of artistic subjectivities in Southeast Asia and their negotiation with the rest of the world. It is interpreted as being less about identifying the Philippines specifically and more about the "complexities of identity-in-becoming" and a "personal revelation of the human condition and spirit."
