# Dexcom (DXCM)

Dexcom is a leading medical device company specializing in [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs)]]. In response to the rise of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Dexcom has adopted an [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] strategy.

Key initiatives and products include:
*   **Positioning CGMs for GLP-1 users:** Actively demonstrating how CGMs are essential for monitoring metabolic health and tracking the efficacy of GLP-1 drug regimens.
*   **[[../sources/connect-study-dexcom.md|CONNECT study]]:** A study (June 2026) that demonstrated CGM use significantly improves time-in-range for Type 2 diabetics even when used alongside GLP-1s.
*   **[[../entities/stelo.md|Stelo]]:** A new sensor aimed at the "preventative metabolic health" space.
*   **[[../entities/nutrisense.md|Nutrisense]] acquisition:** Acquired to create a lifestyle-tracking subscription ecosystem that complements pharmacological weight management, aligning with the trend towards [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystems]].

Dexcom's strategy exemplifies the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]], focusing on making their hardware a data-driven "coach" for drug users.
