# The Journal of Financial Data Science

The Journal of Financial Data Science is a newer, high-signal academic journal that tracks the integration of machine learning, alternative data, and systematic protocols into financial research and [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] frameworks. It focuses on the practical application of data science techniques to financial problems.

**Core Focus Areas:**
*   Machine learning applications in finance (e.g., for prediction, classification, anomaly detection)
*   Utilization of alternative data sources (e.g., satellite imagery, social media sentiment, web scraping)
*   Systematic trading strategies and their implementation
*   Robust [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] methodologies for complex quantitative models
*   Data engineering and infrastructure for financial data
*   Ethical considerations and explainability in financial AI

This journal is highly relevant for researchers exploring the intersection of data science and quantitative finance, particularly in the context of designing and validating advanced options strategies.
