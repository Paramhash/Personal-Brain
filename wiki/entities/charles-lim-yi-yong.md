# Charles Lim Yi Yong

[[Charles Lim Yi Yong]] is a Singaporean artist and a member of the art collective [[tsunamii.net]]. He is known for his contributions to [[Internet Art]] and [[New Media Art]], particularly through the collective's "alpha" series.

Lim, along with [[Woon Tien Wei]] and [[Melvin Phua]], created [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]], a web-based installation exhibited at [[Documenta11]] in 2002. This work involved the artists physically traversing Germany while transmitting locative data, aiming to expose the material and infrastructural realities of the internet. Lim interpreted the geographical consolidation of intellectual resources on German servers for Documenta11 as a form of "cultural colonisation," which the artwork sought to critique.

His work with [[tsunamii.net]] is highlighted in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) as a significant example of early internet art that re-politicizes the internet's dimensions.

## Notes
