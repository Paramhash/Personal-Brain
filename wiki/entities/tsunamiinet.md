# tsunamii.net

[[tsunamii.net]] is a Singapore-based art collective established in 2001. Its core members include artists [[Charles Lim Yi Yong]], [[Woon Tien Wei]], and scientist [[Melvin Phua]]. The collective is known for its [[Internet Art]] and [[New Media Art]] practices, often involving performance and installation.

Their work, particularly the "alpha" series, critically engages with the material and infrastructural dimensions of the internet. A notable piece, [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]], was presented at [[Documenta11]] in 2002. This web-based installation involved the artists trekking across Germany, transmitting locative data to screens, thereby disrupting the perceived seamlessness of the internet and highlighting its physical infrastructure. Earlier iterations, such as *alpha 3.0: GPS piece—a web walkabout* (2001) and *alpha 3.3* (2001), were shown in Singapore.

[[tsunamii.net]]'s work is discussed in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) as a significant example of early internet art in Singapore, representing a "contemporary" approach that works "within" the digital to critique its geopolitical and infrastructural aspects.

## Notes
The collective's critical engagement with internet infrastructure and its political dimensions distinguishes their practice within the broader field of new media art, particularly in the context of Singapore's techno-utopic masterplans.
