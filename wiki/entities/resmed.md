# ResMed (RMD)

ResMed is a leading medical device company specializing in products for treating sleep apnea, COPD, and other respiratory conditions, notably [[../concepts/cpap.md|CPAP]] devices. Initially, ResMed was seen as vulnerable to [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies due to the potential for weight loss to reverse sleep apnea.

However, ResMed has successfully countered this narrative by adopting an [[../concepts/expanded-patient-funnels-medical-devices.md|expanded patient funnels]] strategy. Key aspects of their response include:
*   **Higher CPAP adherence:** Internal data highlighted by CEO Mick Farrell shows that patients on GLP-1s actually exhibit higher CPAP adherence rates.
*   **"Discovery" effect:** The GLP-1 "hype cycle" is bringing millions of obese individuals to doctors for weight-loss prescriptions, leading to the identification and diagnosis of a previously "hidden" patient population for ResMed's sleep devices.

This strategic pivot is a significant part of the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]], demonstrating how companies can leverage indirect benefits from new pharmacotherapies.
