# CBOE Global Markets

## Definition
[[../entities/cboe_global_markets.md|CBOE Global Markets]] is a leading operator of exchanges and global marketplaces for financial derivatives and cash equities. It is particularly well-known for its options exchanges, including the Chicago Board Options Exchange (CBOE), which is the largest options exchange in the U.S.

## Products and Services
*   **Options:** Offers trading in a wide range of [[../concepts/options.md|options]] on equities, indexes, and exchange-traded products.
*   **Futures:** Provides futures contracts on various underlying assets.
*   **Equities:** Operates multiple stock exchanges.
*   **Market Data:** Distributes extensive market data, which is crucial for financial research and analysis.

## Role in Research
Data from [[../entities/cboe_global_markets.md|CBOE Global Markets]] is a primary source for academic and industry research involving [[../concepts/options.md|option prices]] and implied volatility. For example, [[../sources/detecting_stock_market_regimes_lai_2022.md|Lai (2022)]] utilized daily options data from CBOE to extract [[../concepts/option_implied_equity_risk_premium.md|option-implied equity risk premia]] for detecting [[../concepts/stock_market_regimes.md|stock market regimes]].
