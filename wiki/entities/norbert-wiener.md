# Norbert Wiener

[[Norbert Wiener]] (1894–1964) was an American mathematician and philosopher, widely recognized as the founder of cybernetics. His 1948 conception of cybernetics defined it as the science of control and communications in human and nonhuman systems.

In the context of "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), Wiener's work is referenced in relation to [[Gunalan Nadarajan]]'s deployment of the term "[[Cyberarts]]" in Singapore. Nadarajan drew cues from Wiener's cybernetics to describe art that engaged with the increasing cyberneticization of society and the complex technological systems managing information flows.

## Notes
