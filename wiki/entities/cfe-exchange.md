# CFE Exchange

The Cboe Futures Exchange (CFE) is a futures exchange operated by Cboe Global Markets. It is primarily known for listing futures contracts on the Cboe Volatility Index (VIX), often referred to as the "fear index." CFE also provides [[../concepts/span-margin.md|SPAN margin data]] for its listed futures products.
