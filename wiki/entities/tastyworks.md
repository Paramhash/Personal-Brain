# Tastyworks

Tastyworks is a US-based online brokerage firm known for its platform designed for active traders, particularly those focused on options and futures. They provide a suite of trading tools, educational content, and an API for programmatic trading and market data access.

The [[../concepts/market-sessions.md|Market Sessions API]] is one of the services offered by Tastyworks, allowing users to programmatically determine market hours, holidays, and current market status across various [[../concepts/instrument-collections.md|instrument collections]].
