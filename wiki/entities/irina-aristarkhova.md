# Irina Aristarkhova

[[Irina Aristarkhova]] is an artist and scholar whose work is recognized within the discourse of [[Cyberfeminism]]. Her practices in [[Cyberarts]] from 2000s Singapore are acknowledged in Mindy Seu's *Cyberfeminism Index*.

Aristarkhova was involved in the burgeoning [[New Media Art]] scene in Singapore in the early 2000s. She produced new artworks for the *Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology* exhibition in 2001, curated by [[Gunalan Nadarajan]]. She also served as the head of the Cyberarts/Cyberculture Research Initiative (CCRI) at the National University of Singapore's University Scholar's Programme (USP) and lectured on cyberarts courses.

"[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) mentions her as one of the individuals whose departure from Singapore by the mid-2000s contributed to the precariousness of new media art programs, which often relied on institutional support.
