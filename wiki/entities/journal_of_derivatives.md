# Journal of Derivatives (JoD)

The Journal of Derivatives (JoD) is a premier academic journal specifically dedicated to the pricing, design, and trading of derivative securities and structured products. It publishes peer-reviewed research that bridges theoretical derivatives pricing with real-world portfolio implementation.

**Core Focus Areas:**
*   Options and futures pricing models
*   Risk management of derivatives
*   Structured products design
*   Empirical studies on derivatives markets
*   Quantitative strategies involving derivatives

It is considered a high-impact journal for researchers and practitioners looking to track or submit cutting-edge literature in the derivatives domain.
