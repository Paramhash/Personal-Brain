# da Vinci Surgical System

The da Vinci Surgical System is the flagship robotic platform developed by [[../entities/intuitive-surgical.md|Intuitive Surgical]], holding the undisputed market leadership in [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|robotic-assisted surgery]].

## Features and Market Position
*   **Ecosystem**: The *da Vinci* ecosystem, including the newer *da Vinci 5*, benefits from a massive installed base globally.
*   **Market Moat**: Its extensive installed base and long-standing presence create a significant competitive moat.
*   **Price Point**: Initial price points typically range from $1.5M–$2.5M.

The *da Vinci* system is the primary competitor for new entrants like [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]]'s [[../entities/hugo-robotic-system.md|Hugo robotic system]], which seeks to offer an alternative in the growing surgical robotics market. Its success is a prime example of a [[../concepts/razor-and-blade-business-model.md|razor-and-blade business model]] in action, with high-margin recurring revenue from instruments and consumables.
