# Paul Virilio

[[Paul Virilio]] (1932–2018) was a French cultural theorist, urbanist, and aesthetician, known for his writings on speed, technology, and their impact on society and perception (dromology).

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), Virilio's observations on the speed of communication technologies are referenced by [[Gunalan Nadarajan]] in his writing about [[tsunamii.net]]'s artwork [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]]. Nadarajan posits that [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]] seeks to "re-invoke the dimensional and matrix components as markers of physical and geophysical space," thereby disrupting the vaunted efficiency and assumed invisibility of internet infrastructures.

## Notes
[[Paul Virilio]]'s theories on speed and technology provide a critical lens for understanding how rapid technological advancements shape human experience and perception, making his work relevant to analyses of [[Internet Art]] that challenge these very notions.
