# Insulet (PODD)

Insulet is a medical device company known for its tubeless insulin pump system, Omnipod, which is part of its [[../concepts/automated-insulin-delivery-aid-systems.md|Automated Insulin Delivery (AID) systems]]. In response to the rise of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, Insulet is aggressively targeting the Type 2 diabetes segment.

Insulet's strategy involves developing AID systems specifically for Type 2 patients, a cohort that often overlaps with individuals taking GLP-1s. By doing so, they aim to capture patients earlier in the disease progression cycle, aligning with the [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] strategic vector. This approach is a key part of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]], demonstrating how device makers are adapting to complement new pharmacological treatments.
