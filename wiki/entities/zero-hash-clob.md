# Zero Hash CLOB

Zero Hash CLOB refers to a Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) provided by Zero Hash, a regulated digital asset infrastructure provider. It facilitates trading in cryptocurrency markets.

Market sessions for Zero Hash CLOB are available via the `Zero Hash CLOB` [[../concepts/instrument-collections.md|instrument collection]] on the sessions range endpoint of the [[../concepts/market-sessions.md|Tastyworks Market Sessions API]].
