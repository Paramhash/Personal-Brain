# Magnificent 7 Stocks

The **Magnificent 7** refers to a group of highly influential, large-capitalization technology and growth stocks that have a significant weighting within the [[../entities/sp500-index.md|S&P 500 Index]]. These companies are known for their dominant market positions, strong financial performance, and substantial impact on overall market movements.

## Constituent Companies

The Magnificent 7 typically includes:

*   **AAPL** (Apple Inc.)
*   **MSFT** (Microsoft Corp.)
*   **NVDA** (NVIDIA Corp.)
*   **AMZN** (Amazon.com Inc.)
*   **GOOGL** (Alphabet Inc. - Class A)
*   **META** (Meta Platforms Inc.)
*   **TSLA** (Tesla Inc.)

## Impact on GEX Analysis

Due to their massive market capitalization and high weighting in the [[../entities/sp500-index.md|S&P 500]], the [[../concepts/gamma-exposure-gex.md|Gamma Exposure (GEX)]] profiles of the Magnificent 7 stocks are critically important for understanding broader market dynamics.

*   **Disproportionate Influence:** Changes in the individual GEX of these stocks can have a significant impact on the aggregate GEX of the entire [[../entities/sp500-index.md|S&P 500 Index]].
*   **Leading Indicators:** If the GEX of these top-weighted stocks diverges significantly from the GEX of the other 495 stocks in the index, the index itself will often eventually follow the direction indicated by these leaders. This makes them crucial for identifying [[../concepts/gex-divergence-strategies.md|GEX Divergence Strategies]], particularly the [[../concepts/fragility-short-strategy.md|Fragility Short]].

Monitoring the GEX of the Magnificent 7 is an "Expert Tip" for traders looking to capitalize on GEX divergences.
