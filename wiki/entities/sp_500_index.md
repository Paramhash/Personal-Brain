# S&P 500 Index

## Definition
The [[../entities/sp_500_index.md|S&P 500 Index]] (Standard & Poor's 500) is a stock market index that represents the performance of 500 of the largest publicly traded companies in the United States. It is a market-capitalization-weighted index, meaning companies with larger market values have a greater impact on the index's performance.

## Significance
*   **Benchmark:** Widely regarded as one of the best gauges of large-cap U.S. equities and a key indicator of the overall health of the U.S. stock market and economy.
*   **Investment Vehicle:** Serves as the underlying asset for numerous investment products, including exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and [[../concepts/options.md|options]].
*   **Research Focus:** Frequently used in academic research as a proxy for the aggregate U.S. equity market, as seen in studies like [[../sources/detecting_stock_market_regimes_lai_2022.md|Lai (2022)]] which analyzes [[../concepts/options.md|options]] written on the S&P 500 to detect [[../concepts/stock_market_regimes.md|stock market regimes]].
