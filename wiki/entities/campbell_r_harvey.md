# Campbell R. Harvey

Campbell R. Harvey is a distinguished academic and researcher in finance, known for his extensive work on asset pricing, financial econometrics, and the methodology of quantitative research, including [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]].

**Notable Publications:**
*   Co-authored with [[../entities/rob_arnott.md|Rob Arnott]] and [[../entities/harry_markowitz.md|Harry Markowitz]], "[[../sources/arnott_harvey_markowitz_2019_backtesting_protocol.md|A backtesting protocol in the era of machine learning]]" (2019). This paper is a foundational text for constructing rigorous, non-delusional backtests, emphasizing the prevention of biases.
