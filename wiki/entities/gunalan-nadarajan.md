# Gunalan Nadarajan

[[Gunalan Nadarajan]] is a prominent curator and scholar in the field of [[New Media Art]] and [[Cyberarts]]. He played a significant role in fostering new media art practice in Singapore during the early 2000s.

Nadarajan curated the *Cyberarts: Intersections of Art and Technology* exhibition in 2001, which was a concerted effort to "jumpstart" the systematic development of new media art in Singapore. He specifically deployed the term "[[Cyberarts]]" to describe art engaging with the cyberneticization of society, drawing from [[Norbert Wiener]]'s concept of cybernetics. He also helped establish the Cyberarts/Cyberculture Research Initiative (CCRI) at NUS and was involved in the National Arts Council's New Media Arts Fund.

He curated earlier iterations of [[tsunamii.net]]'s *alpha* series, including *alpha 3.3* (2001), and wrote about [[tsunamii.net]]'s [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]] for the [[Documenta11]] guide, drawing on [[Paul Virilio]]'s observations on communication technologies. His essay "Not Modern: Theses on Contemporary Art" advocates for a notion of heterogeneity in understanding [[Contemporary Art in Singapore]], challenging linear art historical trajectories.

## Notes
