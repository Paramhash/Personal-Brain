# Jussi Parikka

[[Jussi Parikka]] is a prominent media theorist known for his work on [[Media Archaeology]]. His elucidation of media archaeology emphasizes investigating new media cultures through insights from past media, focusing on "the forgotten, the quirky, the non-obvious apparatuses, practices and interventions."

Parikka's concept of a wider "[[Strategic Amnesia of Digital Culture]]" is central to the argument presented in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023). This amnesia, he suggests, contributes to the perception of digital culture as perpetually "new" and ahistorical.

The article draws upon Parikka's framework to analyze early [[Internet Art]] from Singapore, such as [[Ocarina (1994)]] by [[Lin Hsin Hsin]] and [[alpha 3.4 (2002)]] by [[tsunamii.net]], aiming to disturb the amnesia surrounding Singapore's new media art histories.
