# Kiasma, Nykytaiteen Museo (Museum of Contemporary Art Kiasma)

## Overview
Kiasma is a contemporary art museum located in Helsinki, Finland. It is part of the Finnish National Gallery and is dedicated to the collection, exhibition, and promotion of contemporary art.

## Role in Context
*   **Exhibition Venue**: Kiasma (Nykytaiteen Museo) hosted [[simryn-gill]]'s exhibition *Self-seeds = Itsekylväytyviä* from 11 September to 8 November 1998. This exhibition is referenced in [[michelle-antoinette]]'s *Reworlding Art History* and highlighted in [[clare-veal]]'s review, indicating Kiasma's role in showcasing international contemporary artists.
