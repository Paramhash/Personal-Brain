# Lin Hsin Hsin Art Museum

The [[Lin Hsin Hsin Art Museum]] is a pioneering virtual museum that emerged online in the mid-1990s. It was scripted, designed, and implemented by Singaporean artist and IT inventor [[Lin Hsin Hsin]], who claimed it to be the first virtual museum in the world.

The museum serves as a platform for [[Lin Hsin Hsin]]'s digital art, including works like [[Ocarina (1994)]]. It appropriates conventions of traditional museological spaces (e.g., galleries, information counters, gift shops) to legitimize [[New Media Art]] and [[Internet Art]]. The museum's longevity and various archived iterations (e.g., on archive.org) are noted as an "archaeological site" for digital works.

The [[Lin Hsin Hsin Art Museum]] is discussed in "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023) as a significant early instance of internet art infrastructure in Singapore, reflecting a "feverish desire to be projected as a cutting-edge development for the world" and a "modernist" approach to digital art.

## Notes
The [[Lin Hsin Hsin Art Museum]] represents a crucial early attempt to establish a digital presence for art, predating widespread institutional engagement with internet art and highlighting [[Lin Hsin Hsin]]'s visionary role.
