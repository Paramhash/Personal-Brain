# Rent Collection History

[[../concepts/rent-collection-history.md|Rent collection history]] refers to a landlord's track record of successfully collecting rental payments from tenants. A near-perfect or consistent rent collection history is a strong indicator of tenant quality, effective property management, and the overall stability of a real estate portfolio.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]] is noted for its near-perfect rent collection history.

## Impact:
*   Contributes to [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]] and predictable income streams.
*   A factor in the [[../research/market-mispricing-of-resilient-assets.md|mispricing]] of REITs.
