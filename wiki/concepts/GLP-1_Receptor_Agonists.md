# GLP-1 Receptor Agonists

GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonists are a class of pharmaceutical drugs originally developed for the treatment of type 2 diabetes. They mimic the action of the natural GLP-1 hormone, which helps regulate blood sugar, slow gastric emptying, and promote satiety.

## Therapeutic Applications

*   **Type 2 Diabetes:** Primary indication, improving glycemic control.
*   **Chronic Weight Management:** Increasingly dominant application, leading to significant weight loss.
*   **Emerging Secondary Benefits:** Clinical trials are demonstrating additional benefits, expanding the addressable market:
    *   Cardiovascular risk reduction
    *   Sleep apnea management
    *   Chronic kidney disease slowing
