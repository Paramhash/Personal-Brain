# Triple-Net Structures

[[../concepts/triple-net-structures.md|Triple-net structures]] refer to a type of commercial real estate lease agreement (NNN lease) where the tenant is responsible for paying not only rent but also the three "nets": property taxes, building insurance, and maintenance costs. This arrangement minimizes landlord expenses and provides highly predictable income streams.

## Impact:
*   A core component of the business model for [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]] like [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]].
*   Contributes to the stability and [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|durability]] of the investment.
