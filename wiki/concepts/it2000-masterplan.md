# IT2000 Masterplan

The [[IT2000 Masterplan]] (1992–99) was a significant national infocommunications masterplan launched in Singapore with the 1992 publication of *A Vision of An Intelligent Island: The IT2000 Report* by the then National Computer Board. Its central aspiration was to transform Singapore into an [[Intelligent Island]].

This masterplan was part of a series of initiatives since the 1980s aimed at building and consolidating technological infrastructures in Singapore. It articulated technology as an enabler of "limitless possibilities in the economy and society," contributing to a linear narrative of technological progress.

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), the [[IT2000 Masterplan]] is highlighted as a crucial backdrop for the emergence of early [[Internet Art]] in Singapore. The works of [[Lin Hsin Hsin]] (e.g., [[Ocarina (1994)]]) and [[tsunamii.net]] (e.g., *alpha 3.3*) are analyzed in relation to the drive to build and consolidate technological infrastructures during this period. The plan's notions of an "electronic creative village" are seen to echo in [[Lin Hsin Hsin]]'s adoption of technological apparatuses.

## Notes
The [[IT2000 Masterplan]] represents a foundational period for Singapore's digital development, directly influencing the environment in which early new media artists operated and shaping the discourse around technology and art.
