# Pulsed-Field Ablation (PFA)

Pulsed-Field Ablation (PFA) is an advanced electrophysiology technique used in [[../concepts/cardiovascular-care.md|cardiac rhythm management]]. It involves using precisely controlled electrical pulses to ablate (destroy) targeted heart tissue responsible for arrhythmias, offering a potentially safer and more effective alternative to traditional thermal ablation methods.

## Market Competition
The PFA space is highly competitive. [[../entities/boston-scientific.md|Boston Scientific]] has been particularly aggressive in taking early market share, pushing companies like [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]] to accelerate the rollout of their own PFA systems, such as *PulseSelect*.
