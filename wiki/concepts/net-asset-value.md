# Net Asset Value (NAV)

[[../concepts/net-asset-value.md|Net Asset Value (NAV)]] is a valuation metric commonly used for REITs and other investment funds. For a REIT, NAV represents the estimated market value of its assets (primarily real estate) minus its liabilities. It is often used to assess whether a REIT's stock price is trading at a premium or discount to the underlying value of its properties.
