# Investment Thesis

An [[../concepts/investment-thesis.md|investment thesis]] is a well-reasoned argument or rationale for making a particular investment. It outlines the core beliefs, assumptions, and expected catalysts that are anticipated to drive the investment's success.

## Components often include:
*   Identification of [[../research/market-mispricing-of-resilient-assets.md|mispricing]] or market inefficiencies.
*   Analysis of [[../concepts/structural-value.md|structural value]] and underlying business models.
*   Identification of specific [[../entities/dollar-general.md|entities]] or [[../entities/agree-realty.md|assets]].
*   Expected [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalysts]].

## Example:
*   The [[../sources/middle-income-america-investment-thesis.md|Middle Income America Investment Thesis]] focuses on [[../concepts/structural-retail-operators.md|structural retail operators]] and [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]].
