# Replacement Value

[[../concepts/replacement-value.md|Replacement value]] in real estate refers to the cost to replace an existing asset with a new one of similar utility and quality at current market prices. When a property's market value is significantly below its replacement value, it can indicate an undervalued asset, as it would be more expensive to build a new comparable property than to acquire the existing one.

## Impact:
*   A factor in the [[../research/market-mispricing-of-resilient-assets.md|mispricing]] of [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]].
*   Contributes to the intrinsic [[../concepts/structural-value.md|structural value]] of real estate assets.
