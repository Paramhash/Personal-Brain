# Multi-Price Flex Pivot

A [[../concepts/multi-price-flex-pivot.md|Multi-Price Flex Pivot]] describes a retail strategy where a company shifts from a rigid, single-price point model to a more flexible [[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|multi-price architecture]]. This allows the retailer to adapt its product mix and pricing to counter inflation, preserve margins, and cater to a broader range of consumer needs, including higher-margin categories.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   Involves [[../concepts/operational-transformation.md|operational transformation]].
*   Increases [[../concepts/average-basket-size.md|average basket size]].
*   Enables absorption of [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|trade-down traffic]] from higher earners.
*   Aims for [[../concepts/free-cash-flow.md|free cash flow]] acceleration.
