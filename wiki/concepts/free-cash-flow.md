# Free Cash Flow (FCF)

[[../concepts/free-cash-flow.md|Free Cash Flow (FCF)]] is a financial metric that represents the cash a company generates after accounting for cash outflows to support operations and maintain its capital assets. It is a measure of a company's financial performance and its ability to generate cash for shareholders, debt reduction, or reinvestment.

## Impact:
*   Predictable FCF acceleration is a [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalyst]] for companies like [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]].
