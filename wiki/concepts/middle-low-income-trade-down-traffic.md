# Middle/Low-Income Trade-Down Traffic

[[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|Middle/low-income trade-down traffic]] refers to the phenomenon where consumers, facing economic pressures like [[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|falling real wages]] or increased cost of living, shift their purchasing habits from higher-priced retailers to discount stores for everyday consumables. This behavior benefits value-oriented retailers.

## Impact:
*   Increases customer volume for [[../concepts/dollar-store-infrastructure.md|dollar store infrastructure]].
*   A key driver of the [[../concepts/squeezed-wallet-flywheel.md|Squeezed-Wallet Flywheel]].
