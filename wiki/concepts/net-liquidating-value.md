# Net Liquidating Value (NLV)

**Net Liquidating Value (NLV)** represents the total value of an account if all positions were to be liquidated at their current market prices. It is a key metric for assessing the current worth of a trading or investment portfolio.

NLV typically includes:
*   Cash balance
*   Market value of all long and short positions (stocks, options, futures, etc.)

It generally excludes pending cash or unsettled funds, which are sometimes tracked separately (e.g., as "total account value" which includes pending cash).

NLV is often used for:
*   Tracking portfolio performance over time.
*   Calculating daily profit and loss.
*   Determining buying power or margin requirements.

For historical tracking of NLV, APIs like the [tastytrade Net Liquidating Value History API](../entities/tastytrade-net-liquidating-value-history-api.md) provide data in formats like [OHLC candlestick format](../concepts/ohlc-candlestick-format.md).
