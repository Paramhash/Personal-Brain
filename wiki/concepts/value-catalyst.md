# Value Catalyst

A [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalyst]] is an event, trend, or strategic action that is expected to unlock or realize the intrinsic value of an investment, leading to an increase in its market price. These catalysts often address the reasons for [[../research/market-mispricing-of-resilient-assets.md|market mispricing]].

## Examples from the Middle Income America Investment Thesis:
*   For [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]]: Stabilization of [[../concepts/supply-chain-input-costs.md|supply chain input costs]] and [[../concepts/digital-private-label-expansion.md|digital/private-label expansion]].
*   For [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree]]: Margin normalization as [[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|multi-price architecture]] hits full deployment scale, driving [[../concepts/free-cash-flow.md|FCF]] acceleration.
*   For [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]]: Structural rotation out of growth equities into defensive income assets, combined with steady mid-single-digit [[../concepts/funds-from-operations.md|FFO]] growth.
*   For [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]]: Short-term macro rate stabilization removing the "bond proxy" penalty, triggering an upward re-rating to [[../concepts/net-asset-value.md|NAV]].
