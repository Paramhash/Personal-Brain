# Options Risk Metrics

Options Risk Metrics are the quantitative measures used to evaluate and compare the risk/reward profile of individual options positions and the portfolio as a whole. These are the primary decision inputs for the Risk Management Team in the [MAOPM system](../research/Current%20Research%20Initiatives.md).

## Per-Position Metrics

### Maximum Loss

The worst-case loss for a position, assuming the underlying moves to the worst possible price at expiration.

| Strategy | Max Loss |
|---|---|
| Long call / put | Premium paid (100% of debit) |
| Short put (CSP) | Strike − premium received (effectively buying stock at the strike) |
| Bull put spread | Spread width − premium received |
| Iron condor | Wider spread width − total premium received |
| Short straddle / strangle | Theoretically unlimited (capped by broker margin) |

**Hard limit principle**: The Risk Management Team enforces a maximum loss per trade as a percentage of portfolio value (e.g., max 2% of portfolio at risk per position).

### Probability of Profit (PoP)

The statistical likelihood that a position expires with a net profit, derived from the implied probability distribution of the underlying at expiration.

**Approximation for short options**: `PoP ≈ 1 − Delta` of the short strike (e.g., a 0.16-delta short put has PoP ≈ 84%).

**Approximation for spreads**: `PoP ≈ 1 − (Net Premium / Spread Width)` for credit spreads.

PoP is inversely related to premium: higher-PoP positions collect less premium and vice versa. The Risk Team weighs PoP against premium capture and expected value when approving trades.

### Expected Value (EV)

```
EV = (PoP × Max Profit) − ((1 − PoP) × Max Loss)
```

A positive EV is a necessary condition for approval; it is not sufficient — the magnitude of max loss and portfolio-level impact must also be within limits.

Note: EV computed from market-implied probabilities already incorporates the [volatility risk premium](../concepts/implied-volatility.md) — IV systematically overstates realized volatility on average, giving premium sellers a structural edge.

### Buying Power Reduction (BPR)

The margin/collateral required by the broker to hold a position. For defined-risk spreads, BPR = spread width × 100 × number of contracts. For cash-secured puts, BPR = strike × 100 × contracts.

BPR is a hard constraint: the Execution Agent verifies available buying power before routing an order. The Risk Team monitors aggregate BPR as a percentage of portfolio net liquidation value (target: < 50% deployed at any time).

### Risk/Reward Ratio

```
Risk/Reward = Max Loss / Max Profit
```

Lower ratios (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1) are typical for high-PoP credit strategies. Higher ratios favor long-vol strategies with lower PoP but larger upside. The Strategy Researchers reference this ratio explicitly in their debate arguments.

## Portfolio-Level Risk Metrics

### Net Portfolio Delta-Dollar Exposure

The dollar change in portfolio value per 1% move in the underlying index. Used alongside [portfolio Greeks management](../concepts/portfolio-greeks-management.md) to size positions relative to total portfolio value.

### Aggregate Max Loss

Sum of max losses across all open positions, net of any hedging relationships. The Risk Team monitors this versus portfolio value — a key "sleep at night" limit.

### Portfolio Concentration Risk

Exposure by underlying, sector, or strategy type. Over-concentration in a single ticker or correlated group increases tail risk. The Risk Team flags when any single underlying exceeds a defined percentage of aggregate BPR.

### Theta-to-Vega Ratio

Ratio of daily theta collected to vega exposure. Indicates the efficiency of premium collection relative to vol risk taken. A healthy short-vol book has high theta relative to vega (low cost of carry per unit of vol risk).

## Metric Interaction

| Condition | PoP | Max Loss | EV | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wide iron condor, high IVR, stable regime | High (75–85%) | Defined (spread width) | Positive | Approve |
| Long straddle, low IVR, vol expansion expected | Lower (40–50%) | Defined (premium paid) | Positive if vol expands sufficiently | Debate-dependent |
| Naked short put, high IVR, negative GEX regime | High (80%+) | Large (strike × 100) | Positive in isolation | Risk Team likely resizes or vetoes due to max loss magnitude |

## Use in MAOPM

These metrics are computed and included in the structured strategy proposal generated by the Portfolio Manager. The Risk Management Team's three perspectives (Aggressive, Conservative, Neutral) each weight these metrics differently — the Conservative perspective anchors on max loss and BPR; the Aggressive perspective anchors on EV and premium capture; the Neutral perspective balances all three.
