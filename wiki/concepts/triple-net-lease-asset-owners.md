# Triple-Net Lease Asset Owners

[[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|Triple-net lease asset owners]] are real estate investment trusts (REITs) or companies that own properties and lease them to tenants under "triple-net" agreements. In a triple-net lease, the tenant is responsible for property taxes, insurance, and maintenance costs, in addition to rent. This structure provides predictable, stable income for the landlord.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   Focus on [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]] and [[../concepts/cost-of-capital-spreads.md|cost-of-capital spreads]].
*   Generate income from rent payments, often from [[../concepts/investment-grade-tenants.md|investment-grade tenants]].
*   Less exposed to operational risks of the underlying retail businesses.
*   Often provide [[../concepts/inflation-insulated-distribution-yields.md|inflation-insulated distribution yields]].
