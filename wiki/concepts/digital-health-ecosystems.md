# Digital Health Ecosystems

Digital health ecosystems refer to integrated platforms and services that combine medical devices, software, data analytics, and connectivity to provide comprehensive health management solutions. These ecosystems often involve wearable sensors, mobile applications, cloud-based data storage, and AI-driven insights.

In the context of the [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]], digital health ecosystems are emerging as a significant "secular tailwind." While [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies disrupt traditional device markets, companies that successfully pair their hardware with data-driven software are seeing their valuations stabilize.

This strategy involves making the device a "coach" for the drug, providing users with insights into metabolic health, treatment efficacy, and lifestyle adjustments. Examples include [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]]'s integration of [[../entities/stelo.md|Stelo]] and [[../entities/nutrisense.md|Nutrisense]] to create a lifestyle-tracking subscription ecosystem that complements pharmacological weight management. This approach aligns with the broader trend of [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] within the medical device industry.
