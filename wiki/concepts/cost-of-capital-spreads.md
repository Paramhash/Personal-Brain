# Cost-of-Capital Spreads

[[../concepts/cost-of-capital-spreads.md|Cost-of-capital spreads]] refer to the difference between the return generated on an investment (e.g., a real estate acquisition) and the cost of the capital used to fund that investment (e.g., debt and equity). A wider spread indicates a more profitable investment. Companies with strong [[../concepts/balance-sheet-playbook.md|balance sheets]] can often achieve wider spreads by accessing cheaper capital.

## Impact:
*   A key focus for [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]] like [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]] and [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]].
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|ADC]]'s pristine balance sheet allows it to widen its investment spread.
