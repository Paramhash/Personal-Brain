# Dollar Store Infrastructure

[[../concepts/dollar-store-infrastructure.md|Dollar store infrastructure]] refers to the extensive network of discount retail locations, particularly those operated by companies like [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]] and [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree]]. This infrastructure is strategically positioned to capture local market share, especially from [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|middle/low-income trade-down traffic]], and often serves as essential local access to consumables in underserved areas.

## Key Characteristics:
*   Often characterized by [[../concepts/geographic-insulation.md|geographic insulation]] in rural areas.
*   Benefits from [[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|macro constraints]] on consumer spending.
