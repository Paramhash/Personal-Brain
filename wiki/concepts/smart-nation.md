# Smart Nation

"[[Smart Nation]]" is a national initiative launched in November 2014 by Singapore's prime minister, Lee Hsien Loong. It envisions citizens "empowered to live meaningful and fulfilled lives, enabled seamlessly by technology, offering exciting opportunities for all."

[[Smart Nation]] is the latest in a series of technological masterplans that have proliferated in Singapore since the 1980s, following earlier initiatives like the [[IT2000 Masterplan]] and [[Intelligent Island]]. These plans consistently employ rhetoric that articulates high-tech urbanity, progress, and efficiency as means of survival and development for Singapore.

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), the [[Smart Nation]] initiative is cited as a contemporary example of Singapore's ongoing techno-utopic narratives. The article notes an artist duo's desire "to insert the ‘art' into Singapore's Smart Nation initiative," highlighting the continued relevance of technology in shaping artistic discourse and national identity.

## Notes
The [[Smart Nation]] initiative exemplifies Singapore's continuous efforts to integrate technology into all facets of society, perpetuating a discourse of "newness" that can contribute to the [[Strategic Amnesia of Digital Culture]] regarding earlier technological and artistic histories.
