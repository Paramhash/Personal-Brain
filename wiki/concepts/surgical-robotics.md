# Surgical Robotics

Surgical robotics refers to the use of robotic systems to assist surgeons in performing medical procedures. This field is a rapidly scaling market, estimated to reach over $60 billion in the next decade.

## Key Systems and Players
*   **[[../entities/da-vinci-surgical-system.md|da Vinci Surgical System]]**: The undisputed market leader, developed by [[../entities/intuitive-surgical.md|Intuitive Surgical]].
*   **[[../entities/hugo-robotic-system.md|Hugo Robotic System]]**: [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]]'s challenger system, aiming for market share with a modular, cart-based approach.
*   **Ottava Robotic Platform**: Under development by [[../entities/johnson-and-johnson.md|Johnson & Johnson]]'s MedTech division.

## Market Dynamics
The market is characterized by intense competition, with companies vying for institutional hospital budgets. Strategies often involve offering systems at various price points and leveraging recurring revenue from consumables and service agreements, indicative of a [[../concepts/razor-and-blade-business-model.md|razor-and-blade business model]].
