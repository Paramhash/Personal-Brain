# Dividend Increases

[[../concepts/dividend-increases.md|Dividend increases]] refer to a company's practice of consistently raising the amount of dividend paid to its shareholders over time. A long history of consecutive dividend increases is often seen as a strong indicator of financial health, stable earnings, and a commitment to returning value to investors.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]] has a 30-plus year history of consecutive annual dividend increases.

## Impact:
*   A key characteristic of a [[../concepts/pure-play-yield-engine.md|Pure-Play Yield Engine]].
*   Contributes to the attractiveness of [[../concepts/inflation-insulated-distribution-yields.md|inflation-insulated distribution yields]].
