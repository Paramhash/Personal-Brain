# Single-Tenant Retail Properties

[[../concepts/single-tenant-retail-properties.md|Single-tenant retail properties]] are commercial real estate assets leased to a single retail occupant. These properties are often subject to long-term [[../concepts/triple-net-structures.md|triple-net leases]], where the tenant is responsible for most property expenses, providing stable and predictable income for the landlord.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]] focuses entirely on these types of properties.

## Impact:
*   Contributes to the [[../concepts/pure-play-yield-engine.md|Pure-Play Yield Engine]] model.
*   Enhances [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]].
