# Funds From Operations (FFO)

[[../concepts/funds-from-operations.md|Funds From Operations (FFO)]] is a key performance metric used by Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs). It adjusts net income by adding back depreciation and amortization (which are non-cash expenses) and subtracting gains from property sales. FFO is considered a more accurate measure of a REIT's operating performance and cash flow than traditional net income.

## Impact:
*   Steady mid-single-digit FFO growth is a [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalyst]] for [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]].
