# Pure-Play Yield Engine

A [[../concepts/pure-play-yield-engine.md|Pure-Play Yield Engine]] refers to an investment, typically a REIT, that is singularly focused on generating consistent and growing income through its core operations. This often involves a stable portfolio of assets, long-term leases, and a proven track record of [[../concepts/dividend-increases.md|dividend increases]], making it attractive to income-focused investors.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   Focus on [[../concepts/single-tenant-retail-properties.md|single-tenant retail properties]] under [[../concepts/triple-net-structures.md|triple-net structures]].
*   Long history of [[../concepts/dividend-increases.md|dividend increases]].
*   High [[../concepts/rent-collection-history.md|rent collection history]].
*   Potential for re-rating to [[../concepts/net-asset-value.md|Net Asset Value]] (NAV).
