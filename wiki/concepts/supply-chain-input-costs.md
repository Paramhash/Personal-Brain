# Supply Chain Input Costs

[[../concepts/supply-chain-input-costs.md|Supply chain input costs]] refer to the expenses associated with acquiring raw materials, components, manufacturing, logistics, and transportation required to produce and deliver goods to market. Fluctuations in these costs can significantly impact a company's profit margins.

## Impact:
*   Can contribute to [[../concepts/temporary-margin-friction.md|temporary margin friction]].
*   Stabilization is a [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalyst]] for retailers like [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]].
