# Regulation T (Reg-T) Margin

Regulation T (Reg-T) is a set of rules established by the Federal Reserve Board that governs the extension of credit by broker-dealers to customers for the purchase of securities. It primarily dictates the initial margin requirements for securities transactions.

Under Reg-T, investors are typically required to deposit 50% of the purchase price of eligible securities when buying on margin. The remaining 50% can be borrowed from the brokerage firm.

The [[../entities/tastytrade-api-account-status.md|Tastytrade Account Status API]] indicates the margin calculation type for equities via the `equities-margin-calculation-type` field in the [[../concepts/trading-status-object.md|TradingStatus object]].
