# Equity Instrument

An equity instrument, commonly referred to as a stock or share, represents ownership interest in a corporation. Holders of equity instruments are typically entitled to a share of the company's profits (dividends) and have voting rights in corporate decisions.

In the context of the [[../concepts/symbol-data-model.md|SymbolData]] model, `Equity` is one of the possible values for the `instrument-type` field.
