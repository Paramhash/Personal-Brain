# Operational Transformation

[[../concepts/operational-transformation.md|Operational transformation]] involves a significant change in a company's core business processes, strategies, or models to improve efficiency, adapt to market shifts, or achieve new strategic objectives. These transformations often carry execution risk but can unlock substantial value if successful.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]] is undergoing an operational transformation by shifting to a [[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|multi-price architecture]].

## Impact:
*   Aims to preserve unit margins and introduce higher-margin categories.
*   Can lead to increased [[../concepts/average-basket-size.md|average basket size]] and [[../concepts/free-cash-flow.md|free cash flow]] acceleration.
