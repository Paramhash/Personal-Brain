# Medical Device Response to GLP-1s

The advent and widespread adoption of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] (Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists) therapies have prompted a significant strategic shift within the medical device industry. Initially viewed as an "existential threat" to hardware-centric businesses, device makers have largely transitioned from a defensive posture to a proactive **integration strategy**.

Rather than being rendered obsolete, devices are being repositioned as essential **companion tools** for managing the long-term health outcomes of GLP-1 users. This adaptation is occurring along three primary strategic vectors:

1.  **[[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|Ecosystem Integration]]: Becoming the "Data Layer"**
    *   Focus on proving that hardware becomes more effective when paired with pharmacological therapy.
    *   Examples include [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] and [[../entities/abbott.md|Abbott]] positioning [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|CGMs]] for metabolic health tracking alongside GLP-1s, and [[../entities/insulet.md|Insulet]] targeting Type 2 diabetics with [[../concepts/automated-insulin-delivery-aid-systems.md|AID systems]].

2.  **[[../concepts/expanded-patient-funnels-medical-devices.md|Expanded Patient Funnels]]: The "Discovery" Effect**
    *   Leveraging the GLP-1 "hype cycle" to bring previously undiagnosed patients into the healthcare system.
    *   Examples include [[../entities/resmed.md|ResMed]] identifying new sleep apnea patients and orthopedic companies like [[../entities/stryker.md|Stryker]] and [[../entities/zimmer-biomet.md|Zimmer Biomet]] using GLP-1s as a "pre-habilitation" tool for elective surgeries.

3.  **[[../concepts/high-acuity-innovation-medical-devices.md|High-Acuity Innovation]]: Focusing on Complex Interventions**
    *   Mitigating risk by concentrating R&D on high-value, complex procedures where medication alone is insufficient.
    *   Examples include [[../entities/boston-scientific.md|Boston Scientific]] and [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]] shifting towards structural heart technologies like [[../concepts/intravascular-lithotripsy-ivl.md|Intravascular Lithotripsy (IVL)]].

**Summary of Strategic Adjustments:**

| Sector           | Initial Fear                  | Current Response Strategy                                                               |
| :--------------- | :---------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Diabetes Tech**  | GLP-1s replace monitoring/pumps. | Integrate with apps to track drug efficacy/metabolic trends.                              |
| **Sleep Health**   | Weight loss cures apnea.      | Capture "newly diagnosed" patients via increased doctor visits.                           |
| **Orthopedics**    | Fewer surgeries needed.       | Use GLP-1s to qualify high-BMI patients for elective procedures.                          |
| **Cardiovascular** | Reduced procedural demand.    | Focus on high-acuity/complex structural heart innovations.                                |

The "GLP-1 disruption" is largely considered an earnings headwind for low-value commodities (e.g., basic stents, generic blood glucose strips) but a **secular tailwind for [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystems]]**. Companies that can successfully pair their hardware with data-driven software, making the device a "coach" for the drug, are seeing their valuations stabilize and grow.
