# Dividend Yield

[[../concepts/dividend-yield.md|Dividend yield]] is a financial ratio that indicates how much a company pays out in dividends each year relative to its share price. It is calculated by dividing the annual dividend per share by the share price. It is a key metric for income-focused investors.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]: ~4.25% (Mid-2026)
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]: ~5.29% (Mid-2026)
