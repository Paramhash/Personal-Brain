# Occupancy Profile

An [[../concepts/occupancy-profile.md|occupancy profile]] describes the percentage of a real estate portfolio that is currently leased to tenants. A high and stable occupancy profile indicates strong demand for the properties and reliable rental income, contributing to [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]].

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]: ~99.7% (Near Flawless)
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]: ~99.4% (Ultra-Stable)
