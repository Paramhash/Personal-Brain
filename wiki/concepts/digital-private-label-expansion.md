# Digital/Private-Label Expansion

[[../concepts/digital-private-label-expansion.md|Digital/private-label expansion]] refers to a growth strategy where retailers increase their offerings of store-branded (private label) products and/or enhance their digital sales channels. Private-label products typically offer higher profit margins compared to national brands, while digital expansion can broaden reach and improve efficiency.

## Impact:
*   Drives earnings recovery and margin improvement.
*   Acts as a [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalyst]] for companies like [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]].
