# Singapore ONE

[[Singapore ONE]] was a nationwide initiative commercially launched in June 1998, as a development stemming from the [[IT2000 Masterplan]]. Its primary goal was to implement broadband networks across Singapore, providing interactive, multimedia applications and services to homes, schools, and businesses.

This initiative was significant as the first of its scope and depth worldwide, quickly followed by the liberalization of the telecommunications market in January 2000. It contributed to a rapid increase in dial-up internet subscriptions and home internet penetration in Singapore during the late 1990s.

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), [[Singapore ONE]] is mentioned as part of the infrastructural backdrop against which early [[New Media Art]] and [[Internet Art]] practices, such as those by [[tsunamii.net]], emerged. It represents the government's efforts to build and consolidate technological infrastructures, influencing the digital landscape for artists.

## Notes
[[Singapore ONE]] played a crucial role in expanding internet access and capabilities in Singapore, creating a more fertile ground for digital artistic experimentation, even as the broader discourse around new media art remained complex.
