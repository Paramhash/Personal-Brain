# Falling Real Wages / SNAP Cuts

[[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|Falling real wages]] and reductions in federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits represent significant macro constraints on consumer spending power, particularly for middle and low-income households. These factors can trigger shifts in consumer behavior, such as [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|trade-down traffic]] to more affordable retail options.

## Impact:
*   Increases pressure on household budgets.
*   Drives consumers towards value-oriented retailers.
