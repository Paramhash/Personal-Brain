# Bulletproof Land Bank

The [[../concepts/bulletproof-land-bank.md|Bulletproof Land Bank]] concept refers to a portfolio of real estate assets, typically held by [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]], that exhibits exceptional [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]] and generates highly stable, predictable income streams. These assets are often leased to strong tenants under long-term, inflation-protected agreements, making them resilient to economic fluctuations and interest rate changes.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   Focus on [[../concepts/cost-of-capital-spreads.md|cost-of-capital spreads]].
*   High [[../concepts/occupancy-profile.md|occupancy profile]].
*   Strong [[../concepts/balance-sheet-playbook.md|balance sheet playbook]].
*   Generates [[../concepts/inflation-insulated-distribution-yields.md|inflation-insulated distribution yields]].
