# Asset Base Focus

[[../concepts/asset-base-focus.md|Asset base focus]] describes the strategic concentration of a company's investments or operations on a particular type or quality of assets. This specialization can lead to expertise, efficiency, and a distinct competitive advantage.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]] focuses on Tier-1 [[../concepts/investment-grade-tenants.md|Investment Grade Retailers]].
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]] focuses on Regional/National Retail Operators and [[../concepts/single-tenant-retail-properties.md|single-tenant retail properties]].
