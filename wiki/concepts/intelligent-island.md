# Intelligent Island

"[[Intelligent Island]]" was a key aspiration for Singapore under its [[IT2000 Masterplan]] (1992–99). This vision articulated technology as an enabler of "limitless possibilities in the economy and society," aiming to build and consolidate internet infrastructures.

The rhetoric of the [[Intelligent Island]] is characterized by techno-utopic reveries and narratives of national socioeconomic progress, positing an unwavering linearity of technological history. It frames technological developments as essential for Singapore's survival and progress, often through technocratic masterplanning.

In "[[Excavating Amnesia: A Media Archaeology of Early Internet Art from Singapore]]" (2023), the [[Intelligent Island]] rhetoric is discussed as the backdrop against which early [[Internet Art]] in Singapore, such as [[Ocarina (1994)]] by [[Lin Hsin Hsin]], emerged. Lin's celebratory stance towards technological apparatuses and their abilities is seen to echo the notions of an "electronic creative village" promoted by the IT2000 plan.
