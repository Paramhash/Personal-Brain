# Balance Sheet Playbook

A [[../concepts/balance-sheet-playbook.md|balance sheet playbook]] refers to a company's strategic approach to managing its assets, liabilities, and equity. This includes decisions around debt structure, liquidity, and capital allocation, which can significantly impact its ability to fund growth, withstand economic shocks, and optimize its [[../concepts/cost-of-capital-spreads.md|cost of capital]].

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]: High liquidity, opportunistic equity issuance.
*   [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty ($NNN)]]: Long-term fixed debt, zero near-term maturities.

## Impact:
*   Contributes to [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]].
*   Enables companies to acquire cash-flowing assets even in tight capital environments.
