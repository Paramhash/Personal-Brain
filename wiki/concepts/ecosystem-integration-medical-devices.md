# Ecosystem Integration (Medical Devices)

Ecosystem Integration is a strategic vector adopted by medical device companies in response to the rise of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies. This strategy involves repositioning devices not as standalone solutions, but as essential components within a broader healthcare ecosystem, particularly when paired with pharmacological treatments. The goal is to demonstrate that hardware becomes more effective and valuable when integrated, often serving as a "data layer" for drug regimens.

Key examples of this strategy include:

*   **Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs):** Leaders like [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] and [[../entities/abbott.md|Abbott]] are actively positioning [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|CGMs]] as vital for GLP-1 users to monitor metabolic health and track drug efficacy. [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]]'s [[../sources/connect-study-dexcom.md|CONNECT study]] demonstrated improved time-in-range for Type 2 diabetics using CGMs alongside GLP-1s. Furthermore, [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] is expanding into preventative metabolic health with its [[../entities/stelo.md|Stelo]] sensor and the acquisition of [[../entities/nutrisense.md|Nutrisense]], creating a lifestyle-tracking subscription ecosystem.
*   **Insulin Delivery Systems:** Companies such as [[../entities/insulet.md|Insulet]] are targeting the Type 2 diabetes segment more aggressively with [[../concepts/automated-insulin-delivery-aid-systems.md|Automated Insulin Delivery (AID) systems]]. By focusing on this cohort, which often overlaps with GLP-1 users, they aim to capture patients earlier in the disease progression cycle.

This approach highlights the shift towards [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystems]], where devices provide data-driven insights that complement and enhance the effectiveness of pharmacological therapies. This strategy is a core component of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
