# Structural Value

[[../concepts/structural-value.md|Structural value]] refers to the inherent, long-term worth of a business or asset derived from its fundamental characteristics, strategic positioning, and enduring competitive advantages, rather than short-term market fluctuations or sentiment. It emphasizes the underlying resilience and cash-generating capabilities of the system.

## Impact:
*   An [[../concepts/investment-thesis.md|investment thesis]] built around structural value seeks to identify assets that are fundamentally mispriced by the market.
*   Contrasts with short-term liquidity or momentum plays.
