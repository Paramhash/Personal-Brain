# Temporary Margin Friction

[[../concepts/temporary-margin-friction.md|Temporary margin friction]] refers to short-term pressures that reduce a company's profit margins, often due to external economic factors or specific operational challenges. These pressures are considered temporary if underlying structural advantages remain intact and catalysts for recovery are identifiable.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General ($DG)]] experienced margin friction due to the reduction of federal [[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|SNAP benefits]].

## Resolution:
*   Stabilization of [[../concepts/supply-chain-input-costs.md|supply chain input costs]].
*   Expansion of higher-margin offerings like [[../concepts/digital-private-label-expansion.md|digital/private-label expansion]].
