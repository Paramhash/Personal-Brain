# Cardiovascular Care

Cardiovascular care encompasses the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases related to the heart and blood vessels. It is a primary growth engine for major medical technology companies.

## Key Areas
*   **Cardiac Rhythm Management**: Involves devices like pacemakers and defibrillators.
*   **Electrophysiology**: Focuses on the electrical activities of the heart, including advanced procedures like [[../concepts/pulsed-field-ablation.md|pulsed-field ablation (PFA)]].
*   **Structural Heart Valves**: Addressing conditions related to heart valve function.

## Key Players
Major companies operating in this space include [[../entities/medtronic.md|Medtronic]], [[../entities/abbott-laboratories.md|Abbott Laboratories]], and [[../entities/boston-scientific.md|Boston Scientific]], who are in fierce competition for market share and innovation.
