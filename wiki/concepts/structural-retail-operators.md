# Structural Retail Operators

[[../concepts/structural-retail-operators.md|Structural retail operators]] are businesses that derive their value from the direct operation of retail stores, often leveraging specific market conditions or consumer behaviors. They are distinct from asset owners.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General ($DG)]]
*   [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   Directly manage retail infrastructure and sales.
*   Often sensitive to consumer spending patterns, such as [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|trade-down traffic]].
*   May employ strategies like [[../concepts/geographic-insulation.md|geographic insulation]] or [[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|multi-price architecture]].
