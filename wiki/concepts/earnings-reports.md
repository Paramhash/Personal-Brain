# Earnings Reports

**Earnings Reports** are official financial statements issued by public companies, typically on a quarterly and annual basis, detailing their financial performance over a specific period. These reports provide crucial information about a company's revenues, expenses, profits (or losses), and earnings per share (EPS). They are closely watched by investors, analysts, and traders as they can significantly influence a company's stock price.

Key components often found in earnings reports:
*   **Revenue:** Total sales generated.
*   **Net Income:** The company's profit after all expenses and taxes.
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** A company's profit divided by the outstanding shares of its common stock.
*   **Guidance:** Future financial projections provided by the company's management.

**Relevance to Options Trading:**
Earnings announcements are often catalysts for significant price movements in the underlying stock, leading to increased [[./implied-volatility.md|implied volatility]] in the options market leading up to the announcement. This phenomenon is often referred to as "earnings premium." Traders may engage in "earnings plays" by strategizing around these events, either by selling premium when IV is high or by buying options in anticipation of a large move.

The [[../entities/tastyworks-market-metrics-api.md|Tastyworks Market Metrics API]] provides historical earnings data, including the `occurred-date` of the announcement and the reported `eps`, allowing traders to analyze past performance and volatility behavior around these events.
