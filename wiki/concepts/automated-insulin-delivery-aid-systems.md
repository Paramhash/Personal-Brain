# Automated Insulin Delivery (AID) Systems

Automated Insulin Delivery (AID) systems, also known as artificial pancreas systems, are advanced medical devices that integrate an insulin pump, a [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)]], and a control algorithm to automatically adjust insulin delivery based on real-time glucose readings.

In response to the rise of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, companies like [[../entities/insulet.md|Insulet]] are aggressively targeting the Type 2 diabetes segment with AID systems. This strategy aims to capture patients earlier in their disease progression, often the same cohort who are also taking GLP-1s. By providing sophisticated insulin management, AID systems serve as an integral part of the [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] strategy for medical device makers, complementing pharmacological treatments rather than being replaced by them.
