# Inflation-Insulated Distribution Yields

[[../concepts/inflation-insulated-distribution-yields.md|Inflation-insulated distribution yields]] refer to income streams from investments, typically real estate, that are protected from the erosive effects of inflation. This protection often comes from lease agreements with built-in rent escalators or from assets whose value and income generation naturally rise with inflation.

## Impact:
*   A key benefit provided by [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]] like [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]] and [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]].
*   Contributes to the appeal of the [[../concepts/bulletproof-land-bank.md|Bulletproof Land Bank]] concept.
