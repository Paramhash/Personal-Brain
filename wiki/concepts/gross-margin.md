# Gross Margin

Gross margin is a financial metric that represents the percentage of revenue that exceeds the cost of goods sold (COGS). It indicates how efficiently a company is using its labor and supplies in producing its products or services.

## Calculation
Gross Margin = (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) / Revenue

## Significance
While [[../concepts/operating-margin.md|operating margin]] considers all operating expenses, gross margin focuses specifically on the profitability of production. Companies with high gross margins often have strong pricing power, efficient production processes, or a business model that emphasizes high-value components or recurring revenue.

For example, [[../entities/intuitive-surgical.md|Intuitive Surgical]], as a pure-play monopoly in [[../concepts/surgical-robotics.md|surgical robotics]], boasts a commanding gross margin of ~67% to 68%, reflecting the high profitability of its *[[../entities/da-vinci-surgical-system.md|da Vinci]]* system and associated consumables.
