# ISO 8601

**ISO 8601** is an international standard covering the exchange of date- and time-related data. Its primary purpose is to provide a well-defined, unambiguous, and universally understood method for representing dates and times, avoiding misinterpretation when data is exchanged between different countries or systems.

Key features of ISO 8601 include:
*   **Date Format:** `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2023-10-27`)
*   **Time Format:** `HH:MM:SS` (e.g., `14:30:00`)
*   **Combined Date and Time:** `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS` (the `T` is a separator)
*   **Time Zones:** Can include timezone offsets (e.g., `+00:00` for UTC) or a `Z` suffix for UTC. Zoned datetime formats often include the timezone identifier in brackets, like `[UTC]`.

Example of a zoned datetime format as specified by the tastytrade API: `2026-01-01T00:00:00+00:00[UTC]`

APIs, such as the [tastytrade Net Liquidating Value History API](../entities/tastytrade-net-liquidating-value-history-api.md), frequently use ISO 8601 for specifying `start-time` and `end-time` parameters to ensure consistent and accurate date/time handling across different regions and systems.
