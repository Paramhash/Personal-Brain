# Average Basket Size

[[../concepts/average-basket-size.md|Average basket size]] is a retail metric that measures the average total value of items purchased by a customer in a single transaction. Increasing the average basket size is a common strategy to boost revenue and profitability without necessarily increasing foot traffic.

## Impact:
*   A key benefit of [[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|multi-price architecture]] for retailers like [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]].
*   Contributes to [[../concepts/free-cash-flow.md|free cash flow]] acceleration.
