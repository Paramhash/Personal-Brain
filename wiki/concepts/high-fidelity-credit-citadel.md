# High-Fidelity Credit Citadel

A [[../concepts/high-fidelity-credit-citadel.md|High-Fidelity Credit Citadel]] describes a real estate portfolio, typically within the net-lease sector, that is predominantly comprised of properties leased to [[../concepts/investment-grade-tenants.md|investment-grade tenants]]. This focus on high-credit-quality tenants significantly reduces default risk and ensures highly reliable rent collection, making the portfolio exceptionally durable.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty ($ADC)]]

## Key Characteristics:
*   High percentage of rent from investment-grade tenants (e.g., [[../entities/walmart.md|Walmart]], [[../entities/tractor-supply.md|Tractor Supply]], [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]]).
*   Pristine [[../concepts/balance-sheet-playbook.md|balance sheet]] allowing for opportunistic acquisitions.
*   Aims for steady [[../concepts/funds-from-operations.md|funds from operations]] (FFO) growth.
