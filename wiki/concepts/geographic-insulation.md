# Geographic Insulation

[[../concepts/geographic-insulation.md|Geographic insulation]] is a business strategy where a company strategically locates its operations in areas with limited competition, often rural communities. This creates a protective barrier against rivals and allows the company to become the dominant or sole provider of goods and services in that local market.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General ($DG)]] focuses on rural communities with populations under 20,000.

## Impact:
*   Contributes to a [[../concepts/deep-value-rural-monopolist.md|Deep Value Rural Monopolist]] position.
*   Enhances local market share capture for [[../concepts/dollar-store-infrastructure.md|dollar store infrastructure]].
