# Portfolio-Level Durability

[[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|Portfolio-level durability]] refers to the resilience and stability of an entire collection of assets, such as real estate properties, against economic downturns, market volatility, or tenant-specific risks. It is achieved through diversification, high-quality tenants, and robust lease structures.

## Key Characteristics:
*   A core focus for [[../concepts/triple-net-lease-asset-owners.md|triple-net lease asset owners]] like [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]] and [[../entities/nnn-realty.md|NNN Realty]].
*   Contributes to the [[../concepts/bulletproof-land-bank.md|Bulletproof Land Bank]] concept.
*   Often linked to high [[../concepts/occupancy-profile.md|occupancy profile]] and strong [[../concepts/rent-collection-history.md|rent collection history]].
