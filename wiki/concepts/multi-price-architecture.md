# Multi-Price Architecture

[[../concepts/multi-price-architecture.md|Multi-price architecture]] is a retail pricing strategy where a store offers products at various price points, rather than adhering to a single, fixed price. This allows for greater flexibility in product assortment, margin management, and catering to diverse customer segments.

## Example:
*   [[../entities/dollar-tree.md|Dollar Tree ($DLTR)]] is shifting from a $1-only model to offering items at $3 to $5.

## Impact:
*   Allows companies to rapidly adjust product mix to counter inflation.
*   Preserves unit margins and introduces higher-margin categories.
*   Increases [[../concepts/average-basket-size.md|average basket size]].
