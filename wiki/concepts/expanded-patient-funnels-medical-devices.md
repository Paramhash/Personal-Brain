# Expanded Patient Funnels (Medical Devices)

Expanded Patient Funnels is a strategic vector adopted by medical device companies, leveraging the "discovery effect" of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies. This strategy recognizes that the widespread attention and increased doctor visits driven by GLP-1s are bringing millions of previously undiagnosed patients into the healthcare system, creating new opportunities for device makers.

Key examples of this strategy include:

*   **Sleep Apnea:** While initially perceived as vulnerable to GLP-1s due to potential weight-induced apnea reversal, companies like [[../entities/resmed.md|ResMed]] have successfully countered this narrative. Data indicates that patients on GLP-1s show higher [[../concepts/cpap.md|CPAP]] adherence rates. More importantly, the influx of obese individuals seeking weight-loss prescriptions leads to the identification and diagnosis of a previously "hidden" patient population for sleep devices.
*   **Orthopedics:** For companies such as [[../entities/stryker.md|Stryker]] and [[../entities/zimmer-biomet.md|Zimmer Biomet]], GLP-1s are acting as a "pre-habilitation" tool. Patients who were previously deemed too high-risk for elective joint replacement due to high BMI thresholds are now using GLP-1s to reduce their weight and qualify for surgery. This effectively unlocks a previously unreachable surgical backlog, expanding the patient funnel for orthopedic devices.

This strategy demonstrates how device makers are adapting to the GLP-1 landscape by identifying and capitalizing on indirect benefits, turning a potential threat into a growth opportunity. It is a crucial part of the overall [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
