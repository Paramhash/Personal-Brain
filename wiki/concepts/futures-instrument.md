# Futures Instrument

A futures instrument is a standardized legal agreement to buy or sell something at a predetermined price at a specified time in the future. The "something" can be a commodity, financial instrument, or index. Futures contracts are traded on organized exchanges.

In the context of the [[../concepts/symbol-data-model.md|SymbolData]] model, `Future` is one of the possible values for the `instrument-type` field.
