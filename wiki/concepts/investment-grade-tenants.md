# Investment Grade Tenants

[[../concepts/investment-grade-tenants.md|Investment grade tenants]] are companies that have a high credit rating, typically BBB- or higher from agencies like S&P or Moody's. Leasing properties to such tenants significantly reduces the risk of default and ensures a stable, predictable stream of rental income for landlords, particularly in the net-lease sector.

## Examples:
*   [[../entities/walmart.md|Walmart]]
*   [[../entities/tractor-supply.md|Tractor Supply]]
*   [[../entities/dollar-general.md|Dollar General]]

## Impact:
*   Forms the core of a [[../concepts/high-fidelity-credit-citadel.md|High-Fidelity Credit Citadel]] like [[../entities/agree-realty.md|Agree Realty]].
*   Enhances [[../concepts/portfolio-level-durability.md|portfolio-level durability]].
