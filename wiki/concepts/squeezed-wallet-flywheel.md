# Squeezed-Wallet Flywheel

The [[../concepts/squeezed-wallet-flywheel.md|Squeezed-Wallet Flywheel]] describes a dynamic where economic pressures on consumers, such as [[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|falling real wages]] or reduced benefits, lead to increased traffic and market share for discount retailers. This creates a self-reinforcing cycle where these retailers become essential infrastructure for middle and low-income households.

## Components:
1.  **Macro Constraint:** [[../concepts/falling-real-wages-snap-cuts.md|Falling Real Wages / SNAP Cuts]]
2.  **Consumer Response:** [[../concepts/middle-low-income-trade-down-traffic.md|Middle/Low-Income Trade-Down Traffic]]
3.  **Retailer Benefit:** [[../concepts/dollar-store-infrastructure.md|Dollar Store Infrastructure]] captures local market share.
