# Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs)

Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) are medical devices that measure glucose levels in interstitial fluid continuously throughout the day and night. They provide real-time data, trends, and alerts, offering a comprehensive view of a person's glucose profile.

In the context of [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies, CGMs are increasingly positioned as essential [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|companion tools]] for users. They enable individuals to:
*   Monitor metabolic health effectively.
*   Track the efficacy of their GLP-1 drug regimens.
*   Understand the impact of diet and activity on blood sugar levels.

Companies like [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] and [[../entities/abbott.md|Abbott]] are leaders in the CGM market, actively demonstrating how CGM use can improve time-in-range for Type 2 diabetics, even when used alongside GLP-1s. This integration supports the broader trend towards [[../concepts/digital-health-ecosystems.md|digital health ecosystems]] where hardware provides a "data layer" for pharmacological treatments.
