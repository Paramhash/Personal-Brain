# Market Mispricing of Resilient Assets

This research area investigates the phenomenon where certain highly resilient, cash-generating assets are fundamentally mispriced by the market. The hypothesis is that Wall Street may treat these positions as liquidity donor pools to fund other market segments (e.g., mega-cap tech momentum), thereby creating attractive entry points for investors focused on [[../concepts/structural-value.md|structural value]].

## Key Questions:
*   What specific factors contribute to the market's undervaluation of these assets?
*   How do macro institutional outflows from sectors like Real Estate impact the pricing of fundamentally strong companies?
*   What are the identifiable [[../concepts/value-catalyst.md|value catalysts]] that could lead to a re-rating of these assets?
*   How can the [[../concepts/replacement-value.md|replacement value]] and [[../concepts/net-asset-value.md|Net Asset Value]] (NAV) of real estate assets be better incorporated into market pricing?
