# Southeast of Now: Directions in Contemporary and Modern Art in Asia

## Overview
*Southeast of Now: Directions in Contemporary and Modern Art in Asia* is a peer-reviewed academic journal dedicated to publishing research and critical discourse on art from Southeast Asia. It covers both contemporary and modern art practices, histories, and theories within the region.

## Role in Context
*   **Publication Venue**: This journal published [[clare-veal]]'s review article, "Michelle Antoinette, Reworlding Art History: Contemporary Southeast Asian Art after 1990," in Volume 1, Number 2, October 2017. It serves as a key platform for scholarly discussions on Southeast Asian art.
