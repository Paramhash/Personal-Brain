# Deep learning in the stock market—a systematic survey of practice, backtesting, and applications (Olorunnimbe & Viktor, 2022)

**Title:** Deep learning in the stock market—a systematic survey of practice, backtesting, and applications
**Authors:** [[../entities/kenniy_olorunnimbe.md|Kenniy Olorunnimbe]] & [[../entities/herna_viktor.md|Herna Viktor]]
**Year:** 2022
**Source:** Artificial Intelligence Review, Vol. 56, No. 3
**DOI:** 10.1007/s10462-022-10226-0

**Core Focus:**
While primarily focused on deep learning implementations in the stock market, this comprehensive systematic survey is highly valuable for its detailed breakdown of professional-grade [[../concepts/backtesting.md|backtesting]] methodologies. It addresses critical issues and pitfalls relevant to any complex quantitative model, including those used for options portfolios.

The paper provides insights into:
*   **Execution Traps:** Common mistakes and challenges in implementing trading strategies.
*   **[[../concepts/data_leakage_in_backtesting.md|Data Leakage]] Issues:** How future information can inadvertently contaminate historical simulations.
*   **Performance Metrics:** A structured taxonomy of how to evaluate complex quantitative models using historical multi-frequency market data.
*   **Applications:** A survey of deep learning applications across various financial tasks.

This research serves as an excellent guide for understanding the rigorous protocols required to build reliable and non-delusional backtests, which is crucial for validating [[../concepts/systematic_options_strategies.md|systematic options strategies]].

**Key Takeaways:**
*   Comprehensive overview of deep learning applications in finance.
*   Detailed discussion of professional-grade backtesting methodologies.
*   Emphasis on identifying and mitigating execution traps and data leakage.
*   Taxonomy of performance metrics for quantitative models.
