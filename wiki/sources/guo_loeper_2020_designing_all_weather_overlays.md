# Designing All-Weather Overlays — A Study on Option-Based Systematic Strategies (Guo & Loeper, 2020)

**Title:** Designing All-Weather Overlays — A Study on Option-Based Systematic Strategies
**Authors:** [[../entities/ivan_guo.md|Ivan Guo]] & [[../entities/gregoire_loeper.md|Gregoire Loeper]]
**Year:** 2020
**Source:** SSRN Electronic Journal
**DOI:** 10.2139/ssrn.3688843

**Core Focus:**
This paper provides an empirical template for the structural design of [[../concepts/systematic_options_strategies.md|systematic options strategies]], specifically focusing on [[../concepts/option_overlays.md|option overlays]]. It examines strategies that involve systematically selling options (to harvest the [[../concepts/volatility_risk_premium.md|volatility risk premium]]) paired with downside protection via out-of-the-money (OTM) puts.

The research offers data-driven benchmarks on how various design choices affect overall portfolio return distributions over multi-year cycles, including:
*   **Rebalancing Frequencies:** How often positions are adjusted.
*   **Compounding Frequencies:** How profits are reinvested.
*   **Delta/Strike Selection:** The choice of option strike prices relative to the underlying.
*   **Option Maturities:** The time to expiration of the options used.

The paper contributes to understanding how to construct robust, "all-weather" options strategies that can perform across different market regimes, balancing premium collection with [[../concepts/tail_risk_modeling.md|tail-risk]] mitigation.

**Key Takeaways:**
*   Empirical framework for designing systematic option selling strategies.
*   Importance of combining premium harvesting with downside protection.
*   Analysis of how structural parameters influence portfolio outcomes.
