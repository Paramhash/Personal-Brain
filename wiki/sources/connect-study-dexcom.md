# CONNECT Study (Dexcom)

The CONNECT study, conducted by [[../entities/dexcom.md|Dexcom]] (results published June 2026), is a significant piece of research demonstrating the efficacy of [[../concepts/continuous-glucose-monitors-cgms.md|CGM]] use in conjunction with [[../concepts/glp-1s.md|GLP-1]] therapies.

The study specifically showed that CGM use significantly improves time-in-range for Type 2 diabetics, even when these patients are also using GLP-1s. This finding supports the strategic positioning of CGMs as essential companion tools for GLP-1 users, reinforcing the [[../concepts/ecosystem-integration-medical-devices.md|ecosystem integration]] strategy adopted by medical device makers. The CONNECT study provides evidence for the value proposition of devices within the evolving landscape shaped by new pharmacotherapies, as part of the broader [[../concepts/medical-device-response-to-glp-1s.md|medical device response to GLP-1s]].
