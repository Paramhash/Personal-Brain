# Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism

## Overview
The *Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism* is a comprehensive online reference work dedicated to the study of modernism across various disciplines, including art, literature, architecture, music, and philosophy. It provides scholarly articles and resources on key figures, movements, and concepts of the modernist era.

## Role in Context
*   **Editorial Contribution**: [[clare-veal]], the author of the review article, served as the subeditor for the "Asian Art" section of the *Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism* (2016). This highlights her expertise in Asian art and the encyclopedia's coverage of global modernism.
