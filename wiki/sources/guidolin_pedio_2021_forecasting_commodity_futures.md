# Forecasting Commodity Futures Returns with Stepwise Regressions: Do Commodity-Specific Factors Help? (Guidolin & Pedio, 2021)

**Authors:** Massimo Guidolin and Manuela Pedio
**Publication Year:** 2021
**Journal:** *Annals of Operations Research*

## Summary
This paper by [[../entities/massimo_guidolin.md|Massimo Guidolin]] and [[../entities/manuela_pedio.md|Manuela Pedio]] investigates the predictability of commodity futures returns using a stepwise model selection approach. They explore whether incorporating commodity-specific factors improves forecasting performance compared to models based solely on general macroeconomic or financial variables.

## Key Contribution
The research contributes to the literature on forecasting commodity markets by systematically evaluating the role of various predictors and the effectiveness of stepwise regression in selecting optimal forecasting models. It highlights the importance of dynamic model selection in potentially improving out-of-sample forecast accuracy.

## Impact on Forecasting Methodology
The stepwise model selection approach employed in this paper is a robust method for building predictive models, especially when dealing with a large set of potential predictors. This methodology is adopted by [[../sources/detecting_stock_market_regimes_lai_2022.md|Lai (2022)]] in the application section, where it is used to forecast the [[../concepts/equity_risk_premium.md|equity risk premium]] under different [[../concepts/stock_market_regimes.md|equity market regimes]]. The goal is to determine if improved regime detection, particularly through the [[../concepts/horizon_spread_financial.md|horizon spread]], can enhance forecasting performance within such a framework.
