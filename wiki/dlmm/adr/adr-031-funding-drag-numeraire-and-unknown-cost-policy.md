# [[adr-031-funding-drag-numeraire-and-unknown-cost-policy|ADR-031]] — Funding Drag Is Priced in the Pool's Quote Asset, and an Unknown Funding Cost Fails the Gate

- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Status:** Active
- **Context:** Two gaps survived [[adr-020-ev-numeraire-active-bin-pricing|ADR-020]] and [[adr-028-ev-calculator-zero-inventory-support|ADR-028]], both recorded at the time as unratified rather than defended. **(1) Numeraire.** `ev_policy.md` §5a put AMM-facing EV terms in the DLMM pool's quote asset, priced at the active-bin price, and scoped `binance_funding_drag` out explicitly — "a perp exposure priced in the perp's quote asset ... changed only by an ADR that addresses them". `net_ev = projected_dynamic_fees - projected_lvr_quote - binance_funding_drag` therefore subtracts a perp-quote quantity from two pool-quote quantities. On a mainnet SOL/USDC pool hedged on SOL/USDT the two assets nearly coincide and the error is invisible; on the Devnet pool, whose active-bin price is on the order of 6.9 million quote units per SOL against a Binance mark near 112 USDT, the same subtraction is meaningless. **(2) Unknown cost.** `placeholderBinanceFundingDrag()` returned `0` whenever the hedge snapshot was missing or its mark unusable. Funding drag is a *cost*; setting an unknown cost to zero raises `net_ev` and lets the gate pass because the pipeline did not know the cost, not because the economics were favourable. [[adr-028-ev-calculator-zero-inventory-support|ADR-028]] introduced that zeroing deliberately, as the price of letting a cold start deploy while the Binance composite was permanently `null` on an idle account — and recorded it as fail-open.
- **Decision 1 (Numeraire):** the funding-drag term is expressed in the **DLMM pool's quote asset**, like every other value term on the payload. The hedged base exposure is valued at `activeBinPriceQuotePerBase(solana)` — the same [[adr-020-ev-numeraire-active-bin-pricing|ADR-020]] conversion the other pool-side terms use, never restated — and multiplied by the Binance funding rate and the horizon's funding periods. The funding **rate** remains a Binance fact and is unchanged: it is a dimensionless fraction, so it carries no numeraire and needs no conversion. `binance.mark_price_t` is no longer an input to this term at all.
- **Decision 2 (Unknown cost):** an uncomputable funding drag makes the EV gate **refuse**, through arithmetic rather than a second rule. `placeholderBinanceFundingDrag()` returns `UNKNOWN_FUNDING_DRAG = Number.MAX_VALUE` when any required fact is absent or unusable — no Solana snapshot, no derivable pool price, no Binance snapshot, a stale Binance snapshot, a non-finite funding rate, or a non-finite product. `net_ev` then evaluates to a large negative finite number and `meetsEvHurdle` refuses on the ratified inequality, with no `if (facts missing)` branch anywhere. This mirrors the `Number.MIN_VALUE` sentinel `placeholderGasFees()` already uses for the same purpose, and is deliberately finite rather than `Infinity` so the payload still serializes to a number in structured telemetry.
- **What counts as known:** a funding rate of exactly `0` is **known**, not unknown — funding is genuinely zero at times, and treating that as missing would refuse a gate on a fact the venue supplied. A **negative** rate stays signed and raises `net_ev`, because a short that is paid funding really does earn it; that benefit is admitted only when every required fact above is valid.
- **Why the numeraire rule is an approximation, stated plainly:** the funding cost is genuinely incurred in the perpetual's quote asset. Converting it into the pool's quote asset exactly would need a perp-quote-to-pool-quote exchange rate, and no admissible source supplies one — importing a third venue's price is the mixing §5 forbids. Valuing the *hedged base quantity* at the pool's own price expresses the same economic exposure in the payload's numeraire, and is exact when the two quote assets are the same or pegged, which is the delta-neutral premise the strategy already rests on. This replaces a dimensional error with a stated modelling assumption, which is a strict improvement and not a claim of exactness.
- **Why fail-closed is affordable now:** [[adr-028-ev-calculator-zero-inventory-support|ADR-028]]'s zeroing existed because the Binance composite was `null` for the life of the process on an idle account — `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` is event-driven and a flat account emits none. The boot-seed change (2026-09-19) removed that cause by seeding the authenticated latch from the [[adr-009-orchestration-loop-architecture|ADR-009]] boot read, and the 15:49 soak confirmed it live with `binance_funding_drag: 0.042417` from a seeded composite. The unknown branch is therefore no longer the normal cold-start path, and making it refuse does not re-block the deployment sequence it was invented to unblock.
- **Consequences:** every value term on `EvDecisionPayload` is now in one numeraire, so `net_ev` is a coherent quantity for the first time. Moving the Binance mark no longer moves any pool-quote term. A tick that cannot establish funding cost refuses instead of deploying against an unmeasured carry. The estimator remains provisional in *magnitude* — the horizon, the rate's persistence and the hedge ratio are all still placeholders — and this ADR ratifies its **units and its failure direction**, not its economics. Scope note: the stale check applies to the Binance snapshot, the fact this term requires; Solana staleness is not made a new gate here, since that would change other terms by implication and belongs to whichever ADR ratifies them.
- **Alternatives rejected:** keep pricing the hedged notional at the perp mark — the dimensional error this ADR exists to remove, and §5a already forbids it for the neighbouring terms; convert perp-quote to pool-quote via a third price source — importing a foreign price is exactly the mixing §5 forbids, performed at the moment the pipeline is least able to notice; refuse whenever the pool quote asset differs from the perp quote asset — correct in principle and unusable in practice, since it would refuse every Devnet run and every non-USDT-quoted pool; return `Infinity` for an unknown cost — arithmetically equivalent but serializes to `null` in structured telemetry, destroying the diagnostic at the moment it matters; carry an explicit typed `unknown` state on the payload — defensible, and rejected as disproportionate: it would widen the shared protocol, its validators, its telemetry projection and every reducer consumer to express what one sentinel already expresses through the ratified inequality; keep zeroing and rely on magnitude arguments — that is an argument about how large the unknown is, not a bound on it.
